Marking the first season with none of the original Season 1 cast remaining, *Elite* Season 6 picks up in the wake of Benjamín's arrest and Samuel's death. As a new group of students arrives at Las Encinas and Isadora opens her own nightclub, flash-forwards to a hit-and-run that leaves Iván in critical condition drive the eight-episode mystery.

*This post contains spoilers for Season 6 of Netflix's Elite.*

*Content note: this recap mentions sexual assault, domestic abuse, a fatal hate crime, drug overdoses, and a drive-by shooting.*

### The Quick Version (TL;DR)
* **The Aftermath:** With Benjamín in prison and Samuel dead, Isadora opens her own nightclub while a viral kiss outs Cruz, leading to a devastating hate crime outside a football stadium.
* **The New Faces:** Fresh students including Nico, Didac, and toxic couple Sara and Raúl shake up the hallways, while Isadora fights for justice against her Ibiza attackers.
* **The Twist:** After a masquerade party, Iván is struck in a hit-and-run. Mencía believes she was driving while drugged, prompting Ari to burn the car and testify for Benjamín's release, though Sara was actually behind the wheel.

---

## A Hit-and-Run and a Viral Scandal

The season opens with a flash-forward to Iván getting struck by a speeding car at night before rewinding to the start of the semester under a new, friendlier headmaster, Virginia. While Benjamín pressures Ari from prison to protect the family's image, Isadora opens her own club, Isadora House, to move past her trauma.

At the grand opening, things quickly spiral:
* **Cruz and Patrick:** After a drunken Cruz kisses Patrick at the club, a video of the moment goes viral. Patrick initially takes the blame to protect Cruz's soccer career before Cruz holds a press conference to own up to it himself, making him the target of vicious homophobic harassment.
* **Isadora's Relapse:** Dismissed by her mother and triggered at the DJ booth, Isadora overdoses on pills before Bilal saves her with an ice bath. She also fires a new club worker, Didac, after he accuses her of lying about her assault.
* **New Faces:** Mencía is drawn to new student Sara, whose controlling boyfriend, Raúl, immediately clocks the attraction. Meanwhile, Ari pursues trans student Nico, stumbling through awkward missteps before the two develop genuine feelings, even as Nico begins dealing hormones to fund his surgery.

## A Hate Crime and a Drive for Revenge

As the school splits over Isadora's assault allegations, Didac realizes he was wrong to doubt her. He secretly records one of her attackers, Javi, confessing in a bathroom, though the audio is inadmissible in court. When Javi temporarily lies and claims Iván instigated the assault, Isadora cuts Iván off before Javi finally comes clean and clears Iván's name.

Tragedy strikes after a football match where Cruz scores the winning goal. A stadium riot spills into the streets, and a group of men beats Cruz to death in a brutal hate crime. 

Grief consumes the student body. At Cruz's memorial, a devastated Iván breaks down during his speech and ends up sleeping with Ari in the club bathroom, where Patrick catches them walking out together. Soon after, Ari discovers she is pregnant with Iván's baby.

## Who Was Really Driving

At a masquerade party at Isadora House, Iván tells Patrick he only wants comfort rather than a relationship, leaving Patrick heartbroken. Meanwhile, Raúl sneaks into the club, spikes Mencía's drink to get her out of the way, and corners Sara, who finally stands up to him and admits she kissed Mencía.

When a drunk Ari tries to drive herself home, a groggy, drugged Mencía takes her own keys to stop her. Moments later, Mencía wakes up inside a wrecked car with a shattered windshield and no memory of the crash, convinced she hit Iván. Ari rushes to the scene, douses the car in gasoline, and burns it to erase the evidence.

To make the investigation disappear, Ari strikes a deal with Benjamín: she, Patrick, and Mencía testify in his favor, securing his release from prison so the Blanco family can leave Spain. Ari also ends her pregnancy with support from her friend Sonia, while police finally catch two of Isadora's attackers attempting to assault another girl at the club, arresting all three men.

In the final twist, a flashback reveals that Sara actually took the wheel from a passed-out Mencía and hit Iván by accident. Instead of protecting her, Raúl wiped the car down and let Mencía take the fall, giving him fresh blackmail to keep Sara trapped in his orbit. As the Blanco family drives out of Madrid and Iván wakes up in the hospital whispering Patrick's name, an unidentified car speeds past Las Encinas and opens fire on the school.

---

## Where We Left Everyone

* **Iván:** Wakes up from his coma in the hospital, saying Patrick's name first.
* **Patrick:** Steps back to give Iván space and leaves Madrid with his family.
* **Ari:** Ends her pregnancy, burns the car to protect Mencía, and testifies to free her father before leaving Spain.
* **Mencía:** Leaves Madrid believing she caused the hit-and-run, unaware Sara and Raúl framed her.
* **Benjamín:** Released from prison after his children testify on his behalf.
* **Sara & Raúl:** Still trapped together, with Raúl holding the hit-and-run over Sara's head.
* **Nico:** Undergoes his surgery with Sonia supporting him through recovery.
* **Isadora & Didac:** All three of Isadora's attackers are arrested, and she grows closer to Didac right as someone opens fire outside Las Encinas.
* **Rocío & Bilal:** Rocío kisses Bilal as the new social circle settles in.
