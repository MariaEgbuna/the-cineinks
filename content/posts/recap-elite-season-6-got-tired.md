Marking the first season with none of the original Season 1 cast remaining, Elite Season 6 picks up in the wake of Benjamín's arrest and Samuel's death. As a new group of students arrives at Las Encinas and Isadora opens her own nightclub, flash-forwards to a hit-and-run that leaves Iván in critical condition drive the eight-episode mystery.

_This post contains spoilers for Season 6 of Netflix's Elite._

_Content note: this recap mentions sexual assault, domestic abuse, a fatal hate crime, drug overdoses, and a drive-by shooting._

---

### A hit and run, then a grand opening

The season opens with a hit and run. Ivan gets struck by a car, and whoever's driving only stops long enough to check the damage before taking off.

From there it cuts to Ari visiting her father in prison. Benjamín's still trying to justify what he did last season, pushing Ari to act normal, go back to school, and keep up appearances, warning her that his lawyers have orders to cut her off completely if she doesn't play along.

There's a new headmaster, Virginia, who trades Benjamín's authoritarian streak for something more welcoming, and Ivan and Patrick can finally be open about their relationship in the halls. Ari starts wondering if she's interested in a guy named Nico. And Isadora's opening her own club, Isadora House, determined to move forward after what happened to her last season and pulling all her friends in for the grand opening.

At the party, Isadora makes a big entrance announcement on the projector screens, name-drops a guest list that includes Cruz, and takes over the DJ booth herself. In the crowd, Mencía has an icy run-in with a girl named Sara and gets introduced to Sara's boyfriend, Raul, who wastes no time pointing out that he knows Mencía has feelings for his girlfriend.

At the DJ booth, someone kisses Isadora out of nowhere, and it sends her spiraling back to what happened last season. She ends up in the bathroom trying to breathe through it. Ivan follows to check on her, and then her mother barges in, offering her pills and promising they'll make her "a brand new woman in no time."

Ari makes a move on Nico, pushing him to get "plenty of practice" being a straight man. Nico calls her out for it, calling her a transphobe, and walks off while she watches, stunned.

Isadora's mother, unbothered by the video still circulating from last season, decides to head back to Ibiza rather than stay one more night. When Isadora tries to talk to her, her mother brushes her off, annoyed by the "negative energy." It sends Isadora into a full panic attack, and she relapses, back on the pills.

Elsewhere, Raul invites Mencía back to his place, but Sara's clearly not thrilled when he shows up loud and messy. Back at the party, Cruz gets out of control on drugs, and things between him and Ivan are already tense before Patrick tries to smooth things over by talking to Cruz directly. Drunk, Cruz makes a move on Patrick, telling him they had real chemistry, and leans in to kiss him right as Ivan walks back into the room. Ivan reads it wrong and storms off, furious.

Isadora's found passed out from an overdose, and Bilal, thinking fast, dunks her in an ice bath to bring her back. She's not grateful, telling him to keep quiet and clean up the mess before he's sent away. Left alone with her, a guy named Didac calls her a spoiled princess and, when things escalate, accuses her of making up her assault entirely, which gets him fired on the spot.

The episode closes with a video of Cruz and Patrick kissing at the club going viral, while a furious, newly fired Didac storms out into the night.

---

### The school takes sides

Nico's parents start worrying about him almost immediately, and a strange flash shows Ari driving near the club the night Ivan got hit. In the present, Ari keeps chasing Nico and finally gets a real date with him. The school splits over what happened to Isadora, with the boys responsible corroborating each other's story well enough that Sara sides with them over her.

The Cruz and Patrick kiss video is still spreading, and Patrick tries to protect Cruz by going live and taking the blame himself, claiming he was the one who pursued Cruz out of starstruck obsession. He tells Ivan afterward that he knows he's ruined things between them, but at least Cruz's name is safer for it. Cruz later tells Ivan the truth, that Patrick sacrificed his own reputation out of love for him. Ivan doesn't want to hear it, telling his father all he ever wanted was a real, present father.

Didac shows up at Isadora's club with the boys who assaulted her, all of them acting like free men. Isadora, cornered, airs her frustration on social media, which brings out a wave of vile comments telling her to kill herself. Didac secretly records one of the boys, Javi, incriminating himself in the bathroom, and brings the recording to Isadora along with an apology for ever doubting her. Around the same time, Ari and Nico's night together falls apart when she hesitates undressing him, and he storms out convinced she's not as accepting as she claims. Sara publicly apologizes for siding with the accused boys, loses over a million followers for it, and fights hard with Raul over the fallout. Cruz, trying to control the narrative around the kiss, holds a press conference and publicly claims responsibility himself.

Isadora's recording turns out to be useless in court since it was captured illegally, and Didac keeps trying to make himself useful to her anyway, mostly getting shut out. Sara's video apology disappears after another blowup with Raul, throwing Ari into a spiral trying to figure out what's true. Mencía senses Sara secretly believes Isadora but can't break free of Raul's control, and steps in enough times to buy her small windows of freedom. Cruz gets sent a chilling message: a mannequin strung up outside his house with "queer" scrawled across it. He tells Patrick to keep it from Ivan.

That night, Isadora's friends gather to unwind, with Bilal reluctantly pulled into the circle. Ari flirts openly with Bilal, which sends Nico storming out jealous, and when Ari follows, he admits he's fallen for her despite her earlier transphobic comment. They fight it out, and Ari ends up going straight to Bilal instead. Mencía notices a bruise on Sara's arm that doesn't match her explanation, and when Raul crashes the party furious, Sara bolts. A fight breaks out on the dance floor between Ivan and Javi, and once the chaos clears, Isadora puts her revenge plan into motion, spiking the drinks of the boys who assaulted her in full view of the club.

### A riot, then a funeral

The next stretch opens on a funeral that looks like it might be Ivan's, though the show holds the reveal for later. Virginia investigates the assault claims directly, and the boys try shifting all the blame onto Ivan. At a football match where Cruz plays, tensions boil over: Nico throws a cruel jab at Ari about Bilal, gets a drink thrown in his face, and then real violence erupts in the crowd once Cruz scores, spilling into a riot with flares and Molotov cocktails outside the stadium. Bilal takes a bottle to the face. That same day, Sara finally leaves Raul, packing her things and running straight to Mencía.

Cruz is jumped by a group of men outside and beaten to death, a hate crime with no ambiguity about the motive. The funeral from earlier turns out to be his, not Ivan's.

In the aftermath, Javi finally cracks and tells Isadora that Ivan was one of the ones who instigated the assault against her, and she pulls away from him entirely, missing even Cruz's funeral. Weeks later, still grieving, the group organizes a memorial for Cruz at the club, and Patrick coaxes Ivan back to school in the meantime. Didac keeps pushing Javi to fully come clean, and Javi eventually does, this time clearing Ivan and confirming he had nothing to do with what happened to Isadora. Isadora, wracked with guilt over turning on him, rushes to Ivan's place to apologize and promise she won't abandon him again.

Raul manipulates his way back into Sara's life with a public livestream vowing to change, guilt-tripping her until she goes back to him despite everything Mencía's tried to warn her about. Nico, denied surgery access by his father until after graduation, starts dealing hormones on the side to fund it himself, striking a deal with two of the boys involved in Isadora's assault. At Cruz's memorial, Ivan breaks down mid-speech, and right after, ends up sleeping with Ari in the club bathroom. Patrick finds them together as they leave the stall. The season then jumps forward to Patrick sitting in a hospital waiting room, having just learned Ivan survived the hit and run, though barely.

### Mencía behind the wheel

Further along, a flash shows Ari pouring gasoline over the car involved in the accident, seemingly trying to cover something up. In the present, Benjamín pushes Ari to get the family to testify on his behalf ahead of his trial, and Mencía refuses outright, telling her sister to stop protecting a man who doesn't deserve it. Ivan, spiraling through grief, ends up at a drugged-out hookup party and nearly sleeps with a masked stranger before realizing it's Patrick, and he blames him for Cruz's death before throwing him out. Nico keeps dealing, and Isadora buys dangerous drugs off him specifically to use against one of her attackers as revenge. Raul's controlling side resurfaces until Sara starts filming him mid-outburst to protect herself. Ari finally visits her father in prison and tells him he let Samuel die and she hopes he rots there.

A flash-forward shows Ari at the hospital after the car's been burned, finding Patrick devastated and terrified he'll never see Ivan again. Back in the present, a masquerade party at the club brings everyone together, with Patrick showing up despite warnings that Ivan needs space, and Ivan recognizing him instantly even behind a mask before walking away. Ari takes a pregnancy test in the club bathroom and finds out she's pregnant with Ivan's baby, and rather than deal with it, drowns it in alcohol. Raul sneaks into the club in disguise hunting for Sara, drugs Mencía's drink to get her out of the way, and corners Sara alone, trying to guilt her one more time about Mencía's "manipulation." Sara finally stands her ground, telling him he has no power over her anymore and revealing she and Mencía have already kissed. Ivan and Patrick try to talk things out, but Ivan makes clear he only wants comfort, not commitment, and a heartbroken Patrick walks away.

Blind drunk, Ari decides to drive herself home. Others notice and alert Mencía, still groggy from the drugged drink, while Isadora pushes Ivan to go find Patrick and be honest about his feelings. In the scramble to stop Ari, Mencía ends up grabbing her own keys instead, and the episode cuts straight to the aftermath of the hit and run: Mencía behind the wheel, waking up dazed with the windshield shattered in front of her.

### Who was really driving

Mencía wakes up to ten missed calls, a smashed windshield, and no clear memory of what happened, only the sick certainty that she's the one who hit Ivan. She calls Ari sobbing, and Ari rushes over to find her sister drugged and barely coherent. Thinking fast, Ari douses the car in gasoline and burns it to the ground. The two of them walk away swearing to keep the whole thing buried.

Ari sends Mencía to sit with Patrick at the hospital while she heads to the prison to see Benjamín. She tells him everything, the hit and run, the cover-up, all of it. He offers to help make it disappear, but only if she convinces her siblings to testify on his behalf in court. Ari's also decided to end her pregnancy, with Sonia offering to be there for her through it.

Things sour between Isadora and Didac once it comes out his family works in the same industry as hers, and it dawns on him that his firing had nothing to do with performance and everything to do with his family trying to muscle into the Spanish market. He tries proving his loyalty to her, but Isadora's exhausted with men at this point and pulls away. Didac tells Javi he regrets staying quiet about what happened to Isadora and should've gone to the police from the start. Hugo's guilt is eating him alive too, and he admits to Alex that the drugs left him unable to perform with anyone. Javi, isolated and unforgiven by the others, watches as Hugo and Alex try to do the same thing to another girl at the club. This time the police catch them in the act before it goes any further. As Javi's taken away too, he tells Isadora to trust Didac, and hopes that someday she can trust men again.

At school, Nico starts second-guessing his surgery, and Sonia, fresh off helping Ari through her abortion, shows up to support him through his own decision too. At the hospital, Ari convinces Patrick to give Ivan space rather than be there when he wakes up, and Patrick leaves him a heartfelt voice message instead. Benjamín's plan works exactly as intended: the kids testify, he walks free, and the family leaves Spain.

But the truth about that night isn't what anyone thinks. Sara was actually the one driving, having taken the wheel from a drugged, spiraling Mencía. She took her eyes off the road for a second, Mencía passed out beside her, and the car hit Ivan by accident. Panicked, Sara called Raul for help, and rather than protect her, he saw an opportunity, wiping the car down and letting the blame fall entirely on Mencía.

The season closes with Raul holding that secret over Sara like a leash, while Ivan finally wakes up in the hospital, the first word out of his mouth being "Patrick." As everyone drives off trying to move forward, another car speeds toward the school, an arm hanging out the back window with a gun firing wildly.

### Where we left everyone

* **Ivan:** Alive in the hospital. Patrick left him a voicemail while he was unconscious, and the first word he says on waking is Patrick's name.
* **Patrick:** Steps back to give Ivan space and leaves Madrid with his family.
* **Ari:** Ends her pregnancy, burns the car to protect Mencía, and helps get her father out of prison. Leaves with the family.
* **Mencía:** Believes she hit Ivan, not knowing Sara and Raul set her up. Leaves Madrid.
* **Benjamín:** Released from prison after his children testify for him.
* **Sara:** Still in Raul's orbit heading into Season 7.
* **Raul:** Back in control, with new blackmail material over Sara.
* **Nico:** Has his surgery, with Sonia by his side in recovery.
* **Isadora:** Hugo and Alex are arrested, and she and Didac start finding their way back to each other, right as someone opens fire outside the school.
* **Rocio:** Kissed Bilal and lives in a mansion.
* **Didac:** Fired at the start of the season, then spends it helping Isadora.
