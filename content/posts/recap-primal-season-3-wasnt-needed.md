Taking a darker supernatural turn, Primal Season 3 opens months after Spear's sacrifice. Revived as an undead warrior with fragmented memories, Spear traverses a hostile world to piece together who he is while Fang and Mira fight to protect their growing families.

_This post contains spoilers for Primal Season 3._

---

## A Shaman brings him back wrong

![RECAP: Primal Season 3](/images/posts/recap-primal-season-3/image-2.jpg)

The season opens on a burning village and a Shaman performing a ritual most people would call cursed. He rips a heart from a dead raider, floods it with magic, and jams it into Spear's corpse. What crawls out of that tomb isn't a man, it's a weapon on a leash, and the Shaman uses him to wipe out an entire raiding party before catching a stray blade himself. The second the Shaman dies, his control over Spear dies with him, and what's left just stands there in the wreckage, twitching, poking at his own exposed brain.

That's the mode Spear spends most of this season in. No language, barely any memory, just flashes triggered by smells, sounds, and colors. A grasshopper's coloring reminds him of Fang. A pack of lions reminds him of nothing at all, they're just an obstacle between him and the next roar he hears in the distance. He gets adopted by a village of leper outcasts who are the only people not afraid of his rotting face, fights off an Arabic raiding party for them, and then wanders off the second he hears Fang's roar again.

In Kingdom of Sorrow, Spear stumbles into an old ruined temple, wakes up an entire den of Barbary lions, and has to fight, flee, drown, and eventually spear his way through what feels like fifty of them just to survive one alpha. He wins, obviously, because being undead means blunt trauma barely registers anymore, but it costs him chunks of his skull and his memory.

## Grassy, and the grief that turns him feral

![RECAP: Primal Season 3](/images/posts/recap-primal-season-3/image-3.jpg)

In episode 3, Spear latches onto a random grasshopper because its coloring reminds him of Fang, and he spends the whole episode obsessively protecting it like it's the last piece of her he has left. When a swarm of cave dwelling creatures kills the grasshopper right in front of him after he already fell into a ravine trying to save it, Spear snaps. What follows is a straight up massacre, crushed skulls, torn limbs, until he's standing in a cave full of bodies holding a dead insect.

## Mira refuses to stop looking

![RECAP: Primal Season 3](/images/posts/recap-primal-season-3/image-4.jpg)

While Spear is out there getting mauled by every creature in a fifty mile radius, Mira is heavily pregnant, mourning him, and trying to build a life. That gets interrupted when Jabu, one of the village kids who has grown close to Fang's two hatchlings, gets snatched by a pack of Andrewsarchus. Mira volunteers for the rescue party without hesitation, pregnancy and all.

The rescue mission turns into a bloodbath. Two of the search party members get torn apart by the Andrewsarchus pack. Just when it looks like Mira, Fang, and the hatchlings are about to get overrun, Spear shows up out of the fog, and Mira comes face to face with the zombified version of the man she buried. Fang isn't happy about it either, and that tension carries the next few episodes.

The reunion isn't clean. Spear can't talk or explain what happened to him. He tries to mime fighting the lions and losing his hand, but it just comes across as unsettling grunting. Fang, who's been Spear's closest bond since episode 1 of the entire series, treats him like a threat for a long stretch, shoving him away, growling at him, even ruining a kill out of spite during one of their fights.

The rescue of the captives in Cavern of Horrors follows. Mira sneaks Jabu and the other captives out on a vine rope while the Andrewsarchus sleep, the rope snaps and sends everyone crashing, and Fang goes toe to toe with the massive white furred matriarch while Spear claws his way out of a pit full of enemies. By the end, he's useful to the group again instead of just a scary stranger who followed them home.

## Driven out with torches and rocks

![RECAP: Primal Season 3](/images/posts/recap-primal-season-3/image-5.jpg)

The gratitude doesn't last. Mira gets seriously hurt during the rescue, and the second Spear carries the group back into her village, the villagers see a rotting corpse walking toward them and lose it. He gets pelted with rocks and driven out of town, twice, the second time by a mob with torches that chases him into the jungle until he sinks into a mud pit to escape. Spear doesn't understand why everyone is so scared of him. He saved these people's kids.

Right after, Spear looks at his reflection in a waterfall and finally sees himself the way everyone else does: a corpse with half a skull and no face left to speak of. He understands the fear, and it breaks something in him. He falls off the waterfall not long after and washes up somewhere entirely new, while Mira and Fang, convinced he killed himself, grieve over losing him a second time.

## The Black River arena

![RECAP: Primal Season 3](/images/posts/recap-primal-season-3/image-6.jpg)

Spear washes up in a volcanic region ruled by the Black River people, a tribe that worships a mysterious black fluid and forces champions to fight to the death in an arena for the right to drink it. Spear gets shoved into the pit almost immediately, and because he's already dead, he demolishes every opponent they throw at him: a giant with a warhammer, a prehistoric monk who manages to wound him once the fluid starts healing his body enough to feel pain again, and a Scotsman on a pterosaur, whose mount and kill Spear ends up stealing in the same fight.

Every drink of that fluid pulls him a little closer to being whole again. His muscles regrow. His eyes clear up. He starts remembering Mira properly, understanding what she was trying to tell him about the pregnancy months earlier.

Meanwhile, back in the village, Mira gives birth to their daughter, and the show cuts between that and Spear beating a man to death in an arena for a cup of black river water. He eventually steals the pterosaur, flies back to sneak a look at his new daughter, and spends the time between fights quietly crafting her a little wooden doll, obsessing over a fallen opponent's carved polearm because it'd make a good toy.

## Coming home to almost lose everyone

![RECAP: Primal Season 3](/images/posts/recap-primal-season-3/image-7.jpg)

The finale stretch throws Spear back together with his family just in time for Fang to get mauled by a werepanther type creature protecting Mira and the baby, which gives us a proper, no hesitation reunion. Spear shows Mira and Fang how much he's healed and gives his daughter the doll he carved for her. For one episode, the family gets a break.

Then the Black River tribe darts the entire group unconscious and drags them back to the arena to force feed everyone the black fluid, including Fang and both her kids. Spear's whole family turns into snarling mutants trying to tear each other apart while a volcano erupts underneath the arena. Spear bounces between saving his daughter from falling into lava, trying to snap Fang out of her trance, and getting nearly killed himself. Fang catches Spear mid fall into the magma, after an entire season of barely tolerating him.

The season closes years later. Fang's kids are grown. Spear's daughter is a teenager riding one of them, roaring alongside her the way Spear and Fang used to. Spear, fully healed and human again, just stands there smiling at the family he nearly lost twice over. It ties directly back into the final scene from the season 2 epilogue.

---

## Where we left everyone

*   **Spear:** Fully restored after months as a zombie chasing scraps of memory back to his family. Ends the season healed, human, and watching his daughter grow up alongside Fang's own children.
*   **Fang:** Spent most of the season hostile toward the zombified Spear before finally accepting him back, then survived the Black River fluid's mutation to keep raising her two kids in Mira's village.
*   **Mira:** Gave birth to Spear's daughter while he was still lost in the wild, led a rescue mission for Jabu while heavily pregnant, and eventually reunited her whole fractured family for good.
*   **Jabu:** Rescued from the Andrewsarchus pack thanks to Mira and Fang's kids, and survives the season without any lasting harm.
*   **The Black River people:** Left behind, likely scattered or dead, after their volcano erupted mid ritual and swallowed most of the arena along with their leader.
