I hated Buddy. And the more I think about it, the more I hate it.

It's a comedy horror film from Casper Kelly, the guy behind the viral Adult Swim short Too Many Cooks. I went in without watching the trailer, which is an unspoken rule of mine, because I wanted something scary for Halloween. It's only 95 minutes long, and it still took me four days to finish. I just kept zoning out.

The movie takes place inside It's Buddy!, a 1990s kids' show hosted by Buddy, a bright orange unicorn who's way less friendly than he looks. A group of kids is trapped in the show, and Grace, a mother from the real world, gets pulled in and has to team up with them to get out.

**A quick heads up: this review has minor spoilers.**

![REVIEW: Buddy (2026)](/images/posts/i-hated-buddy/image-2.jpg)

The opening is great. It's a spot-on roast of shows like Barney, with the sugary sets, the cheesy acting, and the feeling that something's off just out of frame. It's dark, absurd, and funny, and I wish the whole movie had stayed there.

Once it leaves the TV set, it turns into a generic survival horror movie. It's rated R and there's blood everywhere, and I wasn't scared once. The kills are so over the top they're almost silly, and I kept thinking it was made for kids, then I'd remember the R rating and get confused all over again. The first half hour hints that something deeply unsettling is coming, but it never does.

![REVIEW: Buddy (2026)](/images/posts/i-hated-buddy/image-3.jpg)

The pacing doesn't help either. Grace's storyline in the real world slows everything down, because we already know what's happening to the kids and we're just waiting for her to catch up. She's supposed to be the heart of the film, and she has almost nothing to do until the last act. Clint Howard's George is fun, yet the movie does nothing with him. Characters just show up and disappear like the filmmakers were throwing ideas at a wall, and nobody has a real relationship with anybody. This movie just doesn't work.

## Final Verdict

**My Rating: 3.5**

Buddy is more of a comedy than a horror movie, and I think that's where it goes wrong. If you go in wanting scares like I did, you'll be disappointed. If you're fine with weird, dark comedy, you might get more out of it than I did.
