Following the departures of several original characters, *Elite* Season 4 resets Las Encinas under a strict new principal, Benjamín, who arrives with his three children, Ari, Patrick, and Mencía, alongside a European prince. Told through flash-forwards to a New Year's Eve party where Ari is pulled unconscious from a lake, the eight-episode season tracks the new rivalries, relationships, and secrets leading up to the attack.

*This post contains spoilers for Season 4 of Netflix's Elite.*

*Content note: This recap mentions sexual assault, non-consensual filming, and a relationship between an adult and a minor.*

### The Quick Version (TL;DR)
* **The Reset:** Wealthy CEO Benjamín takes over as principal, forcing Samuel and Omar to pass a placement exam to keep their scholarships while his three kids disrupt the school's social order.
* **The Entanglements:** Ari sparks a love triangle between Guzman and Samuel, Patrick drives a wedge between Ander and Omar, and Mencía gets trapped in a dangerous arrangement with an older businessman named Armando.
* **The Finale:** At a New Year's Eve party, Armando beats Ari and leaves her near a lake. Guzman kills Armando with a flare gun, and Guzman, Samuel, and Rebeka dump the body in the water before Guzman and Ander leave Spain to travel.

---

## A New Regime and a Girl in the Lake

Season 4 opens with a flash-forward to New Year's Eve: fireworks go off while Guzman sits frozen in shock and paramedics pull Ari unconscious from a lake, barely alive. Rewinding to the start of the school year, wealthy CEO Benjamín Blanco replaces Azucena as principal and forces Samuel and Omar to pass an advanced placement exam to prove they deserve their scholarships.

Benjamín's three children quickly tangle with the returning students:
* **Ari:** Catches the eye of both Guzman and Samuel, eventually telling them both through tears that she loves them and refuses to choose.
* **Patrick:** Relentlessly pursues Ander, slipping Omar the exam answers to buy a night out with Ander and sparking a brief three-way hookup at a rebellious towel party before Ander and Omar temporarily break up.
* **Mencía:** Falls for Rebeka, though she spirals after a family fallout and accepts money from an older businessman, Armando, after sleeping with him in a luxury hotel.

Meanwhile, exiled European royal Prince Phillipe arrives at Las Encinas with heavy security and a hidden past. He charms Cayetana, who is now working as the school janitor, though she later discovers hidden cameras in his room and learns he is trying to outrun assault rumors from home.

## Dangerous Secrets and Shifting Loyalties

As the semester progresses, every storyline turns toxic. Mencía's arrangement with Armando turns into blackmail after he becomes a school donor and threatens to expose her to her family. When one of Armando's associates ties Mencía up in a hotel room, Rebeka uses a spare key she found and rescues her.

Elsewhere, Guzman breaks up with Nadia over long distance and starts dating Ari, unaware that Ari is also sleeping with Samuel. Phillipe's true colors emerge at a Christmas social event: after Cayetana turns down a fashion designer who demands sexual favors, Phillipe tries to force himself on Cayetana in the back of a limo, forcing her to physically fight her way out. When his mother later tries to buy Cayetana's silence by excusing her son's aggression as biology, Cayetana refuses.

By New Year's Eve, the pressure snaps. Rebeka tells Ari the truth about Mencía and Armando, Ander decides to leave Las Encinas mid-year and reconciles with Omar after Patrick backs off, and Phillipe sends a recorded confession to a past victim before cutting ties with his mother.

## Midnight at the Lake

At the New Year's Eve party, Samuel asks Ari to leave him alone and go back to Guzman, a conversation Guzman misreads from across the room. Furious, Guzman beats Samuel badly inside the club before seeing a text from Ari asking to meet by the lake.

Down by the water, Rebeka catches Armando getting violent with Mencía and fights him off. Watching from nearby, a drunk Ari realizes Rebeka was telling the truth all along. Ari confronts Armando alone and threatens to call her father, prompting Armando to beat her severely until she collapses on the dock, eventually falling into the lake while reaching for her phone.

Guzman arrives, finds Armando hiding in a nearby storage room, and shoots him dead with a flare gun during a struggle. Returning to the dock without realizing Ari is in the water, Guzman panics and runs back inside to get Samuel and Rebeka. They call an ambulance, and Ari survives in critical condition.

To keep Ari safe from the fallout, Guzman, Samuel, and Rebeka dump Armando's body into the lake. At the hospital, Guzman tells Ari he knows she loves Samuel, claims Armando ran away, and says goodbye. Soon after, Mencía confesses everything to Benjamín with Ari by her side, while Guzman and Ander say tearful goodbyes to their friends and leave Spain to travel together.

## Where We Left Everyone

* **Samuel:** Helped dump Armando's body in the lake and stays behind at Las Encinas.
* **Guzman:** Killed Armando to protect Ari, covered it up, and left Spain to travel with Ander.
* **Ander:** Reconciled with Omar, who encouraged him to see the world while promising to wait.
* **Omar:** Stayed behind in Madrid, with Patrick still hovering nearby.
* **Rebeka & Mencía:** Together at the end of the season, though Rebeka is hiding Armando's murder from Mencía.
* **Ari:** Recovering in the hospital after surviving Armando's attack, unaware of what happened to him.
* **Patrick:** Left alone after Ander and Omar got back together.
* **Cayetana:** Refused to cover for Phillipe and turned down hush money from his mother.
* **Phillipe:** Recorded a private confession to a past victim, though his public reputation remains intact.
