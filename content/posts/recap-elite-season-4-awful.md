Following the departures of several original characters, Elite Season 4 resets Las Encinas under a strict new principal, Benjamín, who arrives with his three children, Ari, Patrick, and Mencía, alongside a European prince. Told through flash-forwards to a New Year's Eve party where Ari is pulled unconscious from a lake, the eight-episode season tracks the new rivalries, relationships, and secrets leading up to the attack.

_This post contains spoilers for Season 4 of Netflix's Elite._

_Content note: this recap mentions sexual assault, non-consensual filming, and a relationship between an adult and a minor._

---

### A new regime and a girl in the lake

Season 4 opens with a flash forward to New Year's Eve: fireworks going off in the background while Guzman sits frozen in a chair, and somewhere nearby a girl is pulled from a lake, unconscious, maybe dead. A police officer wants answers he doesn't have yet. Then it rewinds to the start of the school year.

Las Encinas gets a new principal right out of the gate: Benjamín Blanco, a wealthy CEO who fires Azucena on her first day back, then promises the school a "new chapter" built on discipline, excellence, and merit. Samuel and Omar are told they now have to sit an advanced placement exam just to prove they still deserve their scholarships, a hurdle nobody else on scholarship faced before.

Benjamín brings three kids with him, and each one tangles into the group. Ari catches both Guzman's and Samuel's attention immediately. Patrick is openly gay and starts flirting with Ander almost the second they meet, though the two nearly come to blows in class when Patrick defends his father over the exam. Mencía zeroes in on Rebeka, flirting hard enough that Rebeka, despite still refusing to speak to Samuel after last year's betrayal, takes the bait.

The exam sparks a protest, with students sitting in front of the school entrance to block Benjamín and his kids. Mencía, trying to impress Rebeka, joins the sit-in herself. She also hands the group useful intel: Benjamín's one weakness is Ari, the only person he actually listens to. That sends Guzman off to work Ari directly, hoping she'll talk her father into scrapping the exam.

Rebeka and Mencía's flirtation turns into a date, drinks at a club, until Rebeka gets cold feet mid-hookup and bails without explanation. Mencía doesn't make it home until morning, which earns her a furious lecture from her father and siblings.

Guzman takes Ari out too, opening up about losing Marina and Polo, and Ari, who's lost her own mother, meets him with real understanding. She agrees to talk to her father. But it costs Mencía: cut off financially by Benjamín after she blows off a family dinner, she retaliates by telling Ari that Guzman only took her out to manipulate the exam situation.

Mencía spirals from there, wandering into a bar alone after Rebeka stands her up, where an older man named Armando buys her drinks and, it turns out, a lot more. Meanwhile Patrick offers Omar the exam answers in exchange for Ander joining him at a gay club that night. Omar hesitates, but Ander goes anyway. Samuel makes his own desperate move, cornering Benjamín at the restaurant where he bartends to beg him to drop the exam requirement for Omar, and gets fired on the spot. Benjamín almost softens, until Ari talks him out of it.

At the club, things between Ander and Patrick heat up fast, right up until Patrick tries to go further and Ander shuts it down, insisting he'd never betray Omar, and storms out. Across town, Armando reveals he's a wealthy businessman and offers Mencía a free room in a luxury hotel, no strings attached, or so it seems. She sleeps with him that night and wakes up to find cash left at her bedside.

Exam day arrives, and Patrick slips Omar the answers anyway. Omar uses them, scoring a 97, while Samuel passes on his own merit, and both scholarships are secured. Ari isn't fooled. She pulls Samuel and Guzman aside and makes it clear she sees exactly what they tried to do.

Then it cuts back to New Year's Eve, and the girl in the lake finally has a name. It's Ari. Paramedics work frantically to revive her while everyone watches, frozen, until a voice crackles through on a radio: she's still alive.

### The towel party changes everything

Prince Phillipe's arrival gets treated like a state visit, with security cameras and metal detectors installed just for him, courtesy of a father back home who shipped him off to get away from unspecified rumors. Cayetana's starstruck, though Ari wastes no time outing her as the school janitor in front of him. Phillipe tells her privately he doesn't care about her social class.

Mencía's still seeing Armando behind everyone's back, insisting each time is the last. Sandra, Rebeka's mom, spots the two of them together and warns Mencía directly that Armando is known for going after young girls. Mencía brushes it off.

The Guzman, Samuel, and Ari triangle starts forming. Guzman admits he's attracted to Ari but tells her he's with Nadia, and the tension between him and Samuel over her surfaces quickly. Rebeka gets dragged into her own mess when a new screening policy forces her to disclose her mother's criminal record to the entire class. Mencía comforts her with a kiss in the locker room, and Benjamín catches them and now looks at Rebeka like a delinquent. Stung by the rejection that follows, Mencía goes right back to texting Armando.

The old guard pushes back against the new regime, organizing a rebellious towel party at Samuel's house. Ander, Omar, and Patrick end up in a three-way hookup. Rebeka and Mencía finally get together for real. Guzman video-calls Nadia mid-party, only for her to catch him dancing with Ari on camera. He steps outside to call her back, and the two agree to pause the relationship while they figure out if long distance is sustainable. Ari, rarely one to drink, gets drunk enough to lower her guard and ends up sleeping with Samuel.

Phillipe and Cayetana's hookup that same night gets complicated: the room turns out to be wired with hidden cameras, something Cayetana discovers later, along with a stash of footage showing Phillipe with other women. He explains it away as protection against false assault claims he's dealt with before. The show flashes forward just after to Ari lying in a hospital bed, unconscious and worsening.

Guzman and Nadia officially call it off. Ari suspects Phillipe was with Mencía the night she went missing, and warns Cayetana to be careful of him, though a search online turns up nothing to confirm it.

### Everyone's secrets get dangerous

Mencía's arrangement with Armando spirals. One of his associates gets aggressive with her, tying her down and refusing to let her leave, and it's Rebeka, using a hotel key she'd found earlier and assumed meant Mencía was cheating, who tracks her down and pulls her out. Benjamín finds a stash of cash Mencía's been hiding and jumps straight to blaming Rebeka's family.

Ander breaks things off with Omar, and Omar takes it hard, packing up and moving out before Ander can process it. Guzman keeps circling Ari and Samuel, and a flashforward drops in a strange detail: Samuel gets arrested trying to visit Ari in the hospital.

By episode six, Ari's trying to wall off her private life from Samuel, telling her father never to invite him around again, even as the two keep colliding. Patrick, chasing concert tickets, pushes boundaries harder, at one point groping Ander in the middle of class, which Samuel witnesses and disapproves of. Phillipe, cornered by Cayetana in the janitor's room, asks her outright whether people actually like him or just want what he can give them. His father warns him separately not to let old rumors resurface.

At a family dinner, Guzman, there as Ari's date, compares Mencía to his late sister Marina and quietly needles Benjamín over how he treats his own daughter. Later that night, a drunk Ari initiates sex with Guzman at the club and gets caught by Samuel mid-act. Guzman apologizes and leaves.

Patrick figures out enough about Phillipe's past to use it as leverage, threatening to expose him unless he gets concert tickets for himself and Ander. At the show, Patrick catches Ander kissing someone else and spirals, accidentally busting Ari's lip. Afterward, Ari kisses Samuel again anyway. A flashforward shows Patrick noticing a fresh bruise on Guzman and asking who he'd beaten up.

By episode seven, Phillipe's mother arrives to manage his image and is horrified to learn he's involved with the school janitor. Armando resurfaces as a financial donor to the school, using the access to blackmail Mencía into staying quiet about their arrangement, threatening to expose her to her own family.

The Christmas social event Phillipe invites Cayetana to becomes the season's ugliest turning point. Cayetana meets her dream fashion designer there, only for him to make it clear he'll only look at her work if she goes back to his hotel room. She turns him down, and Ari, watching, gains real respect for her and commissions a dress. Mencía, caught in an almost identical situation the same night, doesn't have the same ending: she gives in to Armando again despite Rebeka begging her not to.

Later that night, alone with Cayetana in a limo, Phillipe pushes past her repeated no, and she has to physically escape the car. He's left shaken, seeming to realize, almost too late, what he's done.

The scene closes with Ari finally coming clean to Samuel and Guzman at once, admitting through tears that she loves them both and doesn't want to choose. Guzman is furious, and Samuel is left stunned.

---

### The fallout before midnight

Guzman doesn't take the news well. He calls Samuel an awful friend and walks off. Not long after, he and Ander sit down for what looks like an early goodbye, Ander having decided to leave Las Encinas mid-year.

Rebeka tells Ari the truth about Mencía and Armando, knowing it likely ends whatever future she and Mencía might've had. Meanwhile Phillipe corners Cayetana in the janitor's room to apologize, but undercuts it by claiming she'd given him "mixed signals" the night before, and she walks off disgusted. When Ari confronts Mencía, Mencía breaks down and threatens to cut Ari off if she takes it to their father.

Learning Ander's actually leaving, Omar asks for one more shot, and after a moment of hesitation, Ander agrees. They kiss, with Patrick watching from a distance, furious.

Phillipe's mother pulls Cayetana into Benjamín's office, trying to buy her silence, dangling the idea that Phillipe chose her over anyone else and that a life with him would end her family's money troubles. She even excuses her son's behavior as simple biology.

### Midnight at the lake

At the New Year's party, the Royal Highness pushes Cayetana one more time to approve of Phillipe publicly. Cayetana refuses outright, telling her that her son's aggression isn't biology, it's how he was raised. Phillipe turns the accusation back on his mother, blaming her for raising him this way. She tells him that if he wants to be treated like an adult, he needs to own the consequences of his own choices.

Back together with Omar, a lighter Ander pushes Guzman to finally talk to Ari, but Samuel beats him to it, asking Ari to leave him alone and go back to Guzman. Guzman sees the exchange from across the room and reads it wrongly. Inside, Patrick watches Ander and Omar dance until he can't anymore and hooks up with a stranger instead.

Omar finds out Ander's real reason for leaving is just to see the world, and instead of holding him back, tells him to go, promising he'll wait. Around the same time, Phillipe records a voice message to a girl named Elodie, someone he'd hurt before, admitting to it and telling her to do whatever she wants with the confession.

Then everything collides. Rebeka catches Armando getting physically aggressive with Mencía down by the lake and attacks him herself to get her out. A drunk, distant Ari watches it happen and realizes Rebeka's been telling the truth the whole time. She confronts Armando alone, threatening to expose him to her father, and starts calling him before Armando cuts her off by hitting her. He keeps hitting her as she tries to crawl toward her phone. At the same moment, Guzman and Samuel boil over into a real fight inside the club, and Guzman beats Samuel badly before seeing a message on his phone from Ari, asking him to meet her by the lake. It all happens as the clock strikes midnight.

Guzman stumbles outside and finds Ari barely conscious on the dock. She begs him to go after Armando. Trying to stand, she reaches for her phone and falls into the lake. Guzman finds Armando in a storage room, and the fight ends with Guzman grabbing a flare gun and shooting him dead. He goes back for Ari, not realizing she's already in the water, and searches the wrong places.

He runs back into the club begging Samuel and Rebeka if they've seen her, breaking down and telling them everything. The three agree to call the police, but with only part of the truth. Ari's rushed to the hospital in critical condition and survives.

Once she's conscious, Guzman sits with her, tells her he already knows she loves Samuel, not him, and lies about what happened to Armando, saying he ran off. He says goodbye as she begs him to stay.

Not long after, Guzman and Ander leave Las Encinas together to travel, saying tearful goodbyes to Omar, Cayetana, Rebeka, and Samuel. Mencía, wracked with guilt over bringing Armando into their lives, asks Ari to stand with her while she tells her father everything, and Benjamín tears his own house apart in rage. In the last reveal of the season, Guzman, Rebeka, and Samuel are the ones who dumped Armando's body into the lake, one more secret the group will have to carry together.

---

### Where we left everyone

*   **Samuel:** Helps dump Armando's body in the lake.
*   **Guzman:** Killed Armando to save Ari, covered it up, and leaves Spain to go travelling with Ander.
*   **Ander:** Learns Omar wants him to go see the world instead of staying behind out of guilt, and leaves, with Omar promising to wait for him.
*   **Omar:** Tells Ander to go live his life and stays behind, with Patrick still hovering around.
*   **Rebeka and Mencía:** End the season together. Rebeka is sitting on the secret of Armando's death without Mencía knowing.
*   **Ari:** Survives Armando's attack and ends up in the hospital, where Guzman lies to her about what actually happened.
*   **Patrick:** Ends the season alone, with Ander and Omar back together.
*   **Cayetana:** Refuses to publicly back Phillipe and turns down his mother's attempt to buy her silence.
*   **Phillipe:** The sexual assault allegations go unresolved, and he ends the season with his reputation mostly intact.
