I built a database to track every movie and show I've watched since 2025. I know how that sounds.

But it means when someone asks me what's been good this year, I don't have to scroll through my brain trying to remember. I just check the database. This isn't a recommendation post. This is me pulling up my own ratings and counting down the ten movies that scored highest for me so far this year.

---

## 10. Dhurandhar (2025) - My rating: 7.7

![Best Movies I've Watched in 2026 So Far (January to June)](/images/posts/best-movies-ive-watched-this-year-so-far/image-2.jpg)

I saw a tweet about this one and figured I'd give it a shot. It's a Bollywood spy thriller from Aditya Dhar, starring Ranveer Singh as an undercover Indian intelligence agent who infiltrates Karachi's criminal underworld. Big, loud, ambitious, and not your typical Bollywood outing.

The ending caught me off guard because it just sort of stops. The story splits into two parts, and the sequel already dropped earlier this year (I haven't gotten to it yet). Fair warning: it runs over three and a half hours, but it's the kind of long where you settle in instead of fighting it.

## 09. End of Watch (2012) - My rating: 7.9

![Best Movies I've Watched in 2026 So Far (January to June)](/images/posts/best-movies-ive-watched-this-year-so-far/image-3.jpg)

My brother put me onto this one. He just said "watch it" and didn't elaborate, so I did. It follows two LAPD partners, played by Jake Gyllenhaal and Michael Pena, patrolling one of the roughest divisions in South Central LA. I'm not rushing back to rewatch it, but it did exactly what it set out to do.

The found footage style takes a minute to adjust to, but once it clicks, the friendship carries the whole thing. Their banter feels completely unscripted, and that sticks with you more than the action does.

## 08. The Gift (2015) - My rating: 8.3

![Best Movies I've Watched in 2026 So Far (January to June)](/images/posts/best-movies-ive-watched-this-year-so-far/image-4.jpg)

I'd heard about this one for years and never got around to it. Watched it this year and holy shit. Jason Bateman and Rebecca Hall play a married couple who run into an old acquaintance from Bateman's character's past, played by Joel Edgerton (who also wrote and directed it, which I didn't know going in).

What starts as an awkward reunion slowly turns into something much darker. The tension builds so gradually you don't notice how uncomfortable you've gotten until the ending hits, and it hits in a way you don't see coming.

## 07. Marty Supreme (2025) - My rating: 8.6

![Best Movies I've Watched in 2026 So Far (January to June)](/images/posts/best-movies-ive-watched-this-year-so-far/image-5.jpg)

This one gave me Uncut Gems vibes immediately, and that's not a coincidence: it's directed by Josh Safdie, solo this time without his brother Benny. It's not on the same level, but the anxiety it creates is the same kind. Timothee Chalamet plays Marty Mauser, a hustler in 1950s New York obsessed with becoming a table tennis champion nobody respects.

It's noisy. There's so much happening on screen at once that my own anxiety spiked, and it took me two days to finish because I needed breaks. That's not a complaint. That's just what this movie does to you.

## 06. No Other Choice (2025) - My rating: 8.8

![Best Movies I've Watched in 2026 So Far (January to June)](/images/posts/best-movies-ive-watched-this-year-so-far/image-6.jpg)

This one starts out as a soft workplace comedy. A man loses his job, his family starts struggling with bills, and then everything switches up so fast you start questioning what you're watching. It's directed by Park Chan-wook, and once you know that, the shift makes sense.

Lee Byung-hun plays a paper industry veteran laid off after 25 years who decides the only way to secure a new job is to eliminate the competition, literally. It's so fucking good. The runtime is a little long, but it's worth every minute once the back half kicks in.

## 05. Transformers One (2024) - My rating: 8.9

![Best Movies I've Watched in 2026 So Far (January to June)](/images/posts/best-movies-ive-watched-this-year-so-far/image-7.jpg)

This is the best Transformers movie ever made, and once you watch it, you'll see I'm not exaggerating. It's an animated prequel about the origin of Optimus Prime and Megatron, back when they were coworkers named Orion Pax and D-16 working in the mines of Cybertron.

The marketing was brutal, the kind that made it look like a kids' movie and tanked it at the box office despite great reviews. The friendship at the center is the real emotional core, and the betrayal hits harder than anything in the live action films. I just hope we eventually get the sequel this story deserves.

## 04. John Wick (2014) - My rating: 9.0

![Best Movies I've Watched in 2026 So Far (January to June)](/images/posts/best-movies-ive-watched-this-year-so-far/image-8.jpg)

This was a rewatch, and according to my database it's near the top of my ratings for the entire year so far. John Wick is so stylish and so confident in what it is. The action choreography still holds up over a decade later, and Keanu Reeves brings a level of physical commitment that elevates the whole thing.

No disrespect to him, but I didn't expect him to actually act in this one, and he does. A simple revenge movie about a man and his dog kicked off one of the most beloved action franchises of the last decade, and watching it again reminds you why.

## 03. Hoppers (2026) - My rating: 9.0

![Best Movies I've Watched in 2026 So Far (January to June)](/images/posts/best-movies-ive-watched-this-year-so-far/image-9.jpg)

I only watched this because of a TikTok edit, which feels like a very 2026 way to discover a Pixar movie. It follows Mabel, a college student who transfers her consciousness into a robotic beaver to communicate with animals and protect their habitat from a mayor's development plans.

I had an absolute blast from start to finish. It gets chaotic about halfway through and never lets up, and the voice cast, especially Piper Curda and Bobby Moynihan, fully commits to the insanity of the premise.

## 02. Predator: Killer of Killers (2025) - My rating: 9.4

![Best Movies I've Watched in 2026 So Far (January to June)](/images/posts/best-movies-ive-watched-this-year-so-far/image-10.jpg)

I'd never seen the original Predator movies. The first one I watched was Prey, and I liked it well enough, so I went into this animated anthology with low expectations. I came out with my mind blown.

It tells three stories across three eras, a Viking raid, a feudal Japan duel between brothers, and a WWII dogfight, all building toward something bigger. The art style is gorgeous: slick, brutal, and confident in a way animated action rarely is. I need more of this.

## 01. The Handmaiden (2016) - My rating: 9.4

![Best Movies I've Watched in 2026 So Far (January to June)](/images/posts/best-movies-ive-watched-this-year-so-far/image-11.jpg)

I'm a little late getting to this one, but I'm glad I finally did. It's told from multiple perspectives, and without spoiling anything, it's a queer love story that actually gets a good ending. That alone makes it worth the watch.

The way the story keeps recontextualizing itself is what makes it unforgettable, and it rewards a rewatch almost immediately, because you start noticing what was hiding in plain sight. I love this movie so much.
