*Blue Eye Samurai* begins in 17th-century Japan, a country closed to the outside world, where Mizu's blue eyes mark her as mixed-race and an outcast. Over eight episodes, she disguises herself as a man and chases revenge, whatever it costs her and the people around her.

*This post contains spoilers for Season 1 of Netflix's Blue Eye Samurai.*

*Content note: This recap mentions graphic violence, torture, drug addiction, sex work, and the killing of a young woman.*

### The Quick Version (TL;DR)

* **The Setup:** Mizu is hunting the white men who were in Japan around the time of her birth, one of whom may be her father. Her trail leads to Abijah Fowler, a smuggler protected inside a fortress.
* **The Mystery:** Can Mizu reach Fowler, and what will she give up to get to him?
* **The Twist:** Fowler attacks the Shogun, and Edo burns. Mizu beats him but keeps him alive, because he can lead her to her other targets in London.

## The Road to Fowler

![Mizu on her hunt for Heiji Shindo](/images/posts/recap-blue-eye-samurai-s1/image-1.jpg "Mizu and Ringo's first meeting (Netflix)")

Mizu's search starts with Heiji Shindo, a man who sells European guns and protects Fowler. She also picks up an unwanted follower: Ringo, a cheerful noodle cook born without hands, who wants to be her apprentice. He eventually wins her over, and he learns that she's a woman.

At the Shindo dojo, Mizu defeats Taigen, a gifted swordsman who bullied her as a child, and cuts off his topknot. Desperate to restore his honor, Taigen follows her. Flashbacks show how Mizu grew up helping Master Eiji, a blind swordsmith, and taught herself to fight.

Taigen is also set to marry Princess Akemi, but her father, Lord Daichi, arranges a match with the Shogun's younger son instead. Akemi runs away, followed by her loyal adviser, Seki.

After Mizu survives the Four Fangs, a band of hired killers, she's badly hurt. Taigen spares her because he wants a proper duel. They later face Heiji together, and Mizu cuts off his hand as they escape his trap. Taigen is later captured and tortured, but he doesn't give her up.

## A Brothel Siege and Mizu's Past

![Mizu defending Madame Kaji's brothel](/images/posts/recap-blue-eye-samurai-s1/image-2.jpg "Mizu and Madame Kaji (Netflix)")

To find a way into Fowler's fortress, Mizu turns to Madame Kaji, who runs a brothel. Kaji's price is the death of a young woman named Kinuyo, which she presents as an act of mercy. Mizu kills her. Akemi, who is searching for Taigen, also crosses her path. She tries to drug Mizu, but ends up tied up.

Boss Hamata, a gang leader, then attacks the brothel, and Mizu fights off his army. Flashbacks reveal that she was once married to Mikio, a disgraced samurai. They grow close, but he turns away after she shows him her skill with a sword. He leaves her to face men who come for her.

It's unclear whether Mikio or Mizu's mother betrayed her. When he returns, he stabs her mother during an argument, and Mizu kills him.

In the present, Mizu hands Hamata over to Kaji and the women. But when Akemi's father's men come to take Akemi home, Mizu refuses to help. Ringo, disappointed, walks away from her.

## The Fortress and the Fire of Edo

![Mizu facing Fowler as fire spreads](/images/posts/recap-blue-eye-samurai-s1/image-3.jpg "Mizu facing Fowler as fire spreads (Netflix)")

Mizu fights through the traps in Fowler's fortress and finds Taigen. Fowler shoots her sword apart, wounding her, and she escapes with Taigen by plunging into icy water. Ringo helps rescue them and gets them to Eiji.

Mizu is as broken as her blade. As she recovers, Fowler prepares to take over from the Shogun with his smuggled guns. Akemi, now in the Shogun's household, begins to find her footing with her husband, Takayoshi. She learns of the plot and takes a warning to her father, only to discover that he's working with Fowler. He locks her up.

Mizu, Ringo and Taigen all end up in the fight for Edo. Seki frees Akemi, while Fowler's forces storm the castle and Fowler kills the Shogun. Taigen kills Heiji. Mizu goes after Fowler and starts a fire to stop him escaping, and the flames spread across the city.

Akemi and Seki try to trap Fowler's army by closing the gates, but Seki is shot dead. Taigen asks Akemi to build a life with him. She turns him down because she wants to be great. Lord Daichi survives the fire badly injured and ends up in Akemi's care.

Mizu finally has Fowler at her mercy. He tells her that her other targets, Skeffington and Routely, are in London, and claims the woman she knew as her mother was really a maid paid to hide her. Her birth mother may still be alive. Ringo goes back to Eiji. Mizu spares Fowler because she needs him, and the season ends with Fowler caged on a ship with her, bound for London.

## Where We Left Everyone

* **Ringo:** He goes back to Master Eiji.
* **Akemi:** She chooses to stay and pursue power within the Shogun's family.
* **Taigen:** He survives the fire, but Akemi turns down a life with him.
* **Lord Daichi:** He survives, badly injured, and is left in Akemi's care.
* **Seki:** He dies helping Akemi.
* **Heiji:** Taigen kills him.
* **Fowler:** He's alive, but he's Mizu's prisoner.
* **Mizu:** She's on her way to London to continue her revenge, with Fowler as her guide.
