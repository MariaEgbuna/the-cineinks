With an innocent man behind bars and Carla holding Polo's murder cover-up together, *Elite* Season 2 introduces three new students who push every secret at Las Encinas toward a breaking point. Framed around police interrogations over Samuel's disappearance, the eight-episode season tracks how far the students will go to bury or expose the truth.

*This post contains spoilers for Season 2 of Netflix's Elite.*

*Content note: This recap mentions a suicide attempt, a sex tape posted without consent, and a half-brother and sister kissing.*

### The Quick Version (TL;DR)
* **The Setup:** With Nano framed for murder, Samuel seduces Carla to get a confession while three newcomers (Rebeka, Valerio, and Cayetana) disrupt Las Encinas.
* **The Mystery:** Samuel vanishes into the woods, triggering a frantic police search and tearing the group apart as old secrets explode at a charity gala.
* **The Twist:** Samuel and Guzman staged the disappearance to break Carla's guilt. Carla finally confesses to the police, only for Cayetana to hide the murder weapon so Polo walks free.

---

## Fresh Blood and Old Guilt

![RECAP: Elite Season 2](/images/posts/recap-elite-season-2/guzman.jpg)

A new semester brings three new faces to Las Encinas, along with a flash-forward showing police searching the woods for a missing Samuel. 

* **Rebeka:** Newly rich after her mother's shady business takes off, she moves into Guzman's foreclosed family mansion and quickly befriends Samuel.
* **Valerio:** Lu's party-loving half-brother enables Guzman's downward spiral while hiding an incestuous relationship with Lu.
* **Cayetana:** A scholarship student pretending to be a wealthy heiress while secretly working alongside her mother as a cleaner.

Meanwhile, the weight of Marina's murder crushes the original group. When a guilt-ridden Christian threatens to go to the police, he gets hit by a car, and Carla's father ships him off to Switzerland to buy his silence. Polo spirals into pill addiction and attempts suicide in the school pool before Cayetana saves him. Before that, Polo confesses the murder to Ander, leaving Ander hollowed out and straining his relationship with Omar, who gets kicked out by his father and moves in with him.

## Samuel's Trap and the Charity Gala

![RECAP: Elite Season 2](/images/posts/recap-elite-season-2/elite-fight.jpg)

Determined to clear Nano, Samuel targets Carla. He confronts her about Marina's death, and their tense dynamic turns into a real, messy affair. Samuel secretly records Carla admitting her role in the cover-up, using the audio to convince a skeptical Guzman that Polo is the real killer.

Everything implodes at Cayetana's charity gala:
* **Secrets Exposed:** After discovering Guzman has been sleeping with Nadia, a humiliated Lu hijacks the microphone and publicly exposes Cayetana's fake wealth, Guzman and Nadia's affair, and Carla's hookups with Samuel.
* **The Fallout:** Out of spite, Lu posts a sex tape of Guzman and Nadia online (filmed earlier by Valerio). When Valerio later exposes his relationship with Lu to their father at Christmas dinner, their father disowns him.

## The Staged Disappearance

![RECAP: Elite Season 2](/images/posts/recap-elite-season-2/s2-finale.jpg)

After the gala, Samuel vanishes, and police find his blood-stained jacket in the woods. Everyone assumes Carla's father or Rebeka's mother had him killed.

In reality, Samuel and Guzman staged the entire disappearance with help from the lead detective. After Samuel narrowly escapes a car chase, Guzman hides him in his grandparents' country house and plants the bloody jacket to rattle Carla's conscience. 

The plan works: believing Samuel is dead, Carla breaks down and confesses everything to the police. Samuel emerges from hiding, Nano is cleared, and Polo is arrested at school. However, Cayetana, who has fallen for Polo, retrieves the murder weapon from the lake and hides it in her closet. Without physical evidence, Polo is released and walks back into Las Encinas for the new term, leaving Guzman furious at Ander for keeping Polo's secret.

## Where We Left Everyone

* **Polo:** Free again. Cayetana hid the murder weapon that would have kept him locked up.
* **Carla:** She finally confessed to the police, only for it to mean nothing.
* **Nano:** Cleared of murder, he packs his bags and leaves town in the rain.
* **Samuel:** Got his brother out of prison, though the scheme cost him whatever he and Carla had.
* **Guzman:** Cut off Ander after learning he covered for Polo, and formed an uneasy truce with Samuel.
* **Ander & Omar:** Back together after Omar learned why Ander shut down and forgave him.
* **Cayetana:** Burned her integrity to protect Polo and keep her spot in the elite circle.
* **Valerio:** Cut off by his father, still causing chaos.
