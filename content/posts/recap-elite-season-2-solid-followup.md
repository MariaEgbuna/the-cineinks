Season 2 picks up directly from season 1 and doesn't let you forget for one second how completely rigged the whole thing was.

Season 1 ended with the truth sitting right there in plain sight, and the wrong person still went to prison for it. Nano's behind bars for a murder Polo committed, Carla's holding the cover-up together, and Samuel's grieving, furious, and running out of options. Into that chaos, Las Encinas welcomes three new students who each bring their own brand of disaster. As usual, flash-forwards drop you into police interrogations while the season walks you toward another body.

*This post contains spoilers for Season 2 of Netflix's Elite.*

*Content note: this recap mentions a suicide attempt, a sex tape posted without consent, and a half-brother and sister kissing.*

---

## How it kicks off

![RECAP: Elite Season 2](/images/posts/recap-elite-season-2/guzman.jpg)

Guzman's family loses their house before the opening credits finish. Rebeka's family, poor turned suddenly rich, moves into it, and she enrolls at Las Encinas the same day Guzman has to walk past his old front door and pretend it doesn't hurt. Still raw over Marina, he attacks Samuel on his first day back. Valerio, Lu's half-brother, shows up not long after and invites the whole school to his VIP table at a club.

Christian is barely holding together. He's the only one who knows Nano is in prison for a murder Polo committed, and Carla has spent the summer trying to keep him distracted. At the club, Christian tells her he's going to the police. She'd already warned her father this might happen. Drunk and furious, Christian storms off on his motorcycle and gets hit by a car that doesn't stop. He survives, and Carla's father shows up at the hospital offering to fly him to Switzerland for treatment, which is really just a payoff dressed up as generosity. Meanwhile, Nadia's father has a stroke back at the family store. Her phone is off, and Ander rushes her to the hospital.

At Valerio's, he and Lu end up drunk and alone, and it tips into a kiss before Lu pulls away and reminds him they swore it would never happen again. Then the episode cuts to a police search moving through a forest, calling out the name of a Las Encinas student who never made it home. It's Samuel.

## Samuel goes after Carla

Samuel decides early that Carla is the way in. At a club he asks if she had something to do with Marina's death, and she says yes. The confession tips straight into them sleeping together, which becomes the pattern: every time he corners her for real answers, it ends the same way, and he wakes up with less information than he wanted.

By episode six, Samuel is confident enough to tell Guzman that Polo killed Marina, with Christian somehow involved too. Guzman nearly starts a fight. When he later finds out Nano went to see Marina's mother to insist on his innocence, he goes looking for blood and catches Samuel in the street. He stops only when Samuel plays back a recorded confession from Carla.

## Everyone falling apart

![RECAP: Elite Season 2](/images/posts/recap-elite-season-2/elite-fight.jpg)

Guzman can't process his sister's death and starts drinking and using with Valerio, who's happy to enable him. At a party he blurts out that Marina was murdered, a seed that starts eating at everyone. Polo is falling apart too: hallucinations from guilt, a pill addiction, and then he tells Ander the truth. Ander is left carrying a secret that wrecks him and his friendship with Guzman. Polo tries to drown himself in the school pool, and Cayetana pulls him out and covers for him without knowing what he's actually guilty of.

Ander and Omar buckle under it. Omar finally snaps at his father, gets thrown out, and moves in with Ander, but Ander can't tell him what's wrong. Nadia and Guzman drift toward each other and sleep together, and Guzman ends things with Lu for good, which she doesn't take well.

Cayetana's double life cracks open. She's been performing wealthy heiress all season while her mother cleans for a living, including Rebeka's mansion and the school. Rebeka confronts her, and Cayetana says nobody was going to hand her the life she wanted, so she built the illusion herself. Polo is the one person who sees the truth, something starts forming between them, and her mother loses her job over Cayetana's stealing.

## The party that blows it up

At the Halloween party, Samuel and Rebeka are piecing together old photos that put Polo in Christian's shirt the night Marina died. Nano, let out for lack of evidence, shows up in a mask and corners Carla, and Samuel beats him badly.

Everything goes down at Cayetana's charity event in episode seven. A small fundraiser meant to quietly bail out her family spirals into a massive gala, and Lu, humiliated after discovering she'd been played, uses her speech to blow up everyone's secrets at once: Cayetana's lie, Guzman and Nadia's affair, and Carla sleeping with Samuel. The party implodes, and Lu later posts a video of Nadia and Guzman having sex online out of spite. Guzman corners Ander and asks point blank if Polo killed his sister. Ander lies to his face. The episode ends with Samuel riding home through the woods at night, a car closing in behind him.

## How it all wraps up

![RECAP: Elite Season 2](/images/posts/recap-elite-season-2/s2-finale.jpg)

The search for Samuel tears the group apart. His bike and blood-soaked jacket turn up in the woods, Carla asks her father outright if he had something to do with it, and Rebeka blames her mother for dragging Samuel into her illegal side business. At Christmas dinner, Lu brags about the gala and Valerio, disgusted, retaliates by outing their relationship to their father, then tells her she's nothing to him now except a sister. Valerio also admits he's the one who recorded the sex tape, and Nadia ends up telling her family a video is out there. They tell her to forget it.

Then the show pulls back the curtain. In flashback, Guzman tells Samuel he believes him, and on his way to meet him, Samuel is chased down a deserted road by men in a car and barely makes it. Guzman hides him, and the two build a plan: Samuel disappears into Guzman's grandparents' country house, and Guzman plants the bloody jacket to pressure Carla into cracking under the guilt. The Inspector eventually gets a warrant, finds Samuel alive in the boiler room, and walks out claiming she saw nothing, because she's in on it too. Carla goes to the police and tells them everything. Samuel comes out of hiding with a flimsy story about a rave, and the truth about the staged disappearance stays buried.

Polo admits the murder to Cayetana, and she doesn't flinch. She repeats his line about second chances back to him and hides the murder weapon in her closet. Polo is arrested at school, and Guzman finds out Ander knew the truth the whole time and cuts him off. In custody, Polo denies everything. Omar is packing to leave Ander for good when he learns the real reason Ander shut down, and instead of walking away, he forgives him.

By the time school resumes after New Year's, Guzman and Samuel seem to have made peace, at least on the surface. Then Polo walks back in, released with no explanation, and only Cayetana looks happy to see him. Behind him, a new student is already talking to the principal.

## Where we left everyone

* **Polo:** Free again. Cayetana hid the evidence that would've kept him locked up.
* **Carla:** She confessed to the police and it meant absolutely nothing, which is its own kind of devastating.
* **Nano:** Cleared, but a wrongful arrest doesn't evaporate. He packs his bags and disappears in the rain.
* **Samuel:** Got his brother out, but the method cost him more than he expected, including whatever he and Carla became.
* **Ander:** Hollowed out all season by Polo's secret, but Omar learns the truth and forgives him. They end the season back together.
* **Cayetana:** Burned her integrity to belong somewhere.
* **Valerio:** Still here, still somehow everyone's problem.

---

## What worked and what didn't

The backbone of the season is Samuel going after Carla, and it was the best thing the show had going.

Christian vanishing partway through bugged me. He gets shipped off to Switzerland after one episode and barely gets mentioned again, which felt like a character getting dropped. Turns out that's scheduling: Miguel Herrán, who plays Christian, was juggling this with Money Heist, and the show wrote him out to make room. It doesn't excuse how abrupt it feels, but it explains it.

The Valerio and Lu thing was rough to watch. They're half siblings, they kiss more than once, and the show treats it like messy sibling chemistry. There's no real reckoning with how wrong it is, just a "we promised we wouldn't do this again" and then more scenes of them being pulled toward each other. I get that the show loves pushing boundaries, but this one was unnecessary.

The show does show how guilt with nowhere to go starts rotting everything around the person carrying it. When Polo confesses to Ander, it feels like relief for about five seconds before you realize what a mess he just made. Watching it hollow Ander out was painful. The warmth drains out of him, and the biggest casualty is his relationship with Omar, taken apart by something Omar isn't allowed to know about.

Cayetana spent the season desperately performing like she belonged at Las Encinas, and she finally becomes one of them by covering up a murder. That's either brilliant writing or a coincidence that worked out perfectly.

## Overall thoughts

Season 2 was decent. It's faster and a little messier than Season 1, but it works more often than it has any right to. You have to accept upfront that none of these people behave like actual teenagers. Make your peace with that and it's a gripping watch.

The question heading into Season 3 is whether the show can hold this tension now that Polo is the known quantity instead of the hidden one. **A secret's only dangerous while it's still a secret.** Now everyone knows. What does the show do with that?

**My Rating: 8.5**
