These are the k-dramas that stuck with me, mostly from the tier just below the mainstream conversation: a thriller that broke my brain, a black comedy nobody talks about, and a superhero show that beat everything Hollywood gave me in years. They're not ranked, and there's one deliberate exception that everyone has already heard of. I'll explain why it's here when we get to it.

## Evilive (2023)

![Kim Young-Kwang in Evilive](/images/posts/kdrama-recommendations-may-27-2026/evilive.jpg)

**2023 · 1 Season · 10 Episodes · Completed**

A struggling lawyer takes on random prison cases to get by. His life spirals out of control when he crosses paths with a ruthless gang leader. Pulled into a dangerous criminal underworld, Dong-soo faces impossible choices that awaken his own dark side. This gritty crime noir explores how far a decent man will go once he crosses the line into evil.

This is the show that fully introduced me to Kim Young-Kwang. I'd seen him in lighter roles here and there. Evilive is where I realized what he's capable of. Since watching this, I've tracked down nearly everything Kim Young-Kwang has been in.

**What could have been better:** The pacing drags a little in the middle, and the plot sometimes leans on coincidence. But the performances carry you through it.

## Strangers from Hell (2019)

![Strangers from Hell](/images/posts/kdrama-recommendations-may-27-2026/strangers-from-hell.jpg)

**2019 · 1 Season · 10 Episodes · Completed**

Aspiring crime writer Yoon Jong-woo moves to Seoul for a new job and settles into Eden Studio, a cheap, rundown apartment building. He quickly realizes his eccentric neighbors are hiding dark and sinister secrets, especially the unsettlingly polite dentist Seo Moon-jo. Trapped by a tight budget and growing paranoia, Jong-woo begins to lose his grip on sanity.

"Eerie" doesn't begin to cover it. This show is unsettling in a way that's hard to describe without spoiling it, and the atmosphere does the heavy lifting before anything explicit happens.

**What could have been better:** The episode count is a little low for the story it wants to tell.

## A Killer Paradox (2024)

![A Killer Paradox](/images/posts/kdrama-recommendations-may-27-2026/image-4.jpg)

**2024 · 1 Season · 8 Episodes · Completed**

Ordinary college student Lee Tang accidentally kills a man during a late-night shift, only to discover his victim was a wanted serial killer. Realizing he possesses an uncanny ability to identify evil people, he becomes an accidental vigilante who escapes justice through sheer luck. Meanwhile, a relentless detective begins piecing together the clues in a tense game of cat and mouse. 

This dark crime thriller explores morality, coincidence, and the blurred line between justice and murder.

**What could have been better:** A few supporting threads are left dangling in a way that feels less like ambiguity and more like the writers ran out of ideas.

## The Kidnapping Day (2023)

![The Kidnapping Day](/images/posts/kdrama-recommendations-may-27-2026/image-5.jpg)

**2023 · 1 Season · 12 Episodes · Completed**

Desperate to pay for his daughter’s hospital bills, clumsy father Kim Myeong-joon attempts to kidnap Choi Ro-hee, an 11-year-old genius from a wealthy family. The plan goes sideways when he accidentally hits her with his car, causing her to wake up with amnesia and mistake him for her dad. Before he can collect the ransom, Myeong-joon uncovers a dark mystery surrounding her family that forces the unlikely pair to go on the run together. 

Blending suspense, sharp humor, and genuine heart, this thriller follows a bumbling kidnapper and a brilliant kid trying to survive both the police and dangerous enemies.

**What could have been better:** The tonal shifts aren't always seamless. It sometimes sits awkwardly between tense and comedic instead of nailing either.

## Moving (2023)

![Moving](/images/posts/kdrama-recommendations-may-27-2026/moving.jpg)

**2023 · 1 Season · 20 Episodes · Completed**

A bunch of high schoolers try to live normal lives while hiding extraordinary superpowers inherited from their parents. As their parents fiercely protect them from being exploited, a mysterious delivery driver begins hunting down people with abilities across Seoul. Forced to confront dangerous threats, the families must unite and embrace their gifts to survive.

Moving is the best thing the superhero genre has produced in years, and I say that as someone who's watched the genre exhaust itself on joyless Hollywood spectacles. The tension runs for twenty episodes without losing steam, and the final stretch is some of the best television I've ever seen. Not best K-drama. Best television. Full stop.

**What could have been better:** Some of the backstory episodes ran a bit too long.

## Flower of Evil (2020)

![Lee Joon-Gi in Flower of Evil](/images/posts/kdrama-recommendations-may-27-2026/image-7.jpg)

**2020 · 1 Season · 16 Episodes · Completed**

Baek Hee-sung is a devoted husband and loving father who hides a dark past under a fabricated identity. His quiet family life begins to unravel when his wife, detective Cha Ji-won, starts investigating a series of unexplained murders that point directly toward her husband. 

Torn between her duty as a cop and her love for her family, Ji-won digs deeper into the truth. This gripping melodrama thriller tests whether love can survive when the person you trust most might be a monster.

**What could have been better:** A tighter edit would have made it flawless instead of just exceptional.

## The Worst of Evil (2023)

![Ji Chang-Wook in The Worst of Evil](/images/posts/kdrama-recommendations-may-27-2026/the-worst-of-evil.jpg)

**2023 · 1 Season · 12 Episodes · Completed**

Set in 1990s Seoul, an undercover detective infiltrates the Gangnam Union, a powerful criminal syndicate running a major international drug trade. To bring down the cartel's ruthless boss, he must earn his trust while hiding his true identity at all costs. The mission turns deeply personal when the detective's wife, an elite narcotics officer, joins the investigation and reveals a past connection to the cartel boss.

**What could have been better:** The show loses its nerve in the final few episodes. The ending pulls toward a cleaner resolution than the story needed, and you can feel the compromise.

## Twinkling Watermelon (2023)

![Twinkling Watermelon](/images/posts/kdrama-recommendations-may-27-2026/image-9.jpg)

**2023 · 1 Season · 16 Episodes · Completed**

After a heated argument with his father, a gifted high school guitarist stumbles into a mysterious music shop and travels back in time to 1995. There, he meets his father as a teenager and joins his band, Watermelon Sugar, determined to protect his future family.

It's the tonal outlier on the list: a time-travel, coming-of-age story centered on music, warmer and stranger than you'd expect.

**What could have been better:** The romance subplot runs longer than necessary, and a few middle episodes meander. The young cast more than makes up for it.

## The Glory (2022)

![Song Hye-Kyo in The Glory](/images/posts/kdrama-recommendations-may-27-2026/the-glory.jpg)

**2022 · 1 Season (2 Parts) · 16 Episodes · Completed**

After surviving brutal high school bullying that shattered her life and dreams, Moon Dong-eun spends nearly two decades carefully plotting her revenge. Taking a job as a homeroom teacher at her former tormentor’s child’s elementary school, she begins dismantling the lives of her privileged abusers one by one. With the help of an unlikely ally hiding his own scars, she turns their deepest secrets into weapons.

This is my one deliberate exception, because The Glory is definitely not *below the mainstream*. It was one of the most talked-about dramas of the last few years. I'm including it because I think the mainstream conversation flattened it into a standard revenge thriller, when it's doing something more precise.

The drama is in the planning, not the execution, and watching her operate like a grandmaster is one of the most satisfying things I've seen. Part 1 alone is close to flawless. If you haven't watched it, close this tab and go fix that. I'll still be here.

**What could have been better:** Part 2 doesn't match the surgical precision of Part 1. The final episodes aren't bad, but you can feel the drop.
