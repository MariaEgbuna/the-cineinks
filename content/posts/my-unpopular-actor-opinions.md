Every fanbase has that group of celebrities everyone loves and you just cannot get the appeal of. This is mine.

---

## The Men

![My Unpopular Actor Opinions](/images/posts/my-unpopular-actor-opinions/image-2.jpg)

**Taron Egerton:** He goes first since he's the easiest to explain. There's nothing wrong with him, he's just missing that extra gear, the thing that makes me sit forward. Black Bird, the Apple TV+ miniseries opposite Paul Walter Hauser, is probably his best work, and even there Hauser was the highlight for me. Kingsman is his biggest commercial win, and he's fine as Eggsy, but he was never the reason people showed up. Carry-On, the Netflix Christmas Eve thriller against Jason Bateman, was one of the platform's biggest debuts in recent memory, which is great for him, but he was the wrong pick for the lead. The role needed someone who could sell quiet desperation, and Egerton plays everything a little too boyish for that tension to build.

![My Unpopular Actor Opinions](/images/posts/my-unpopular-actor-opinions/image-3.jpg)

**Matt Damon:** A different situation. With everyone else on this list I've at least sat through their filmography. Not him. The Bourne movies don't count, since that was my mom's TV choice when I was a kid and I wasn't paying attention. No specific incident, no bad performance I can point to. I just never bothered, and two decades later it's basically permanent.

![My Unpopular Actor Opinions](/images/posts/my-unpopular-actor-opinions/image-4.jpg)

**Michael B. Jordan:** He comes off as a really nice guy every time I see him interviewed, which makes it harder to explain why I don't care more. People bring up Black Panther as his big mainstream moment, but that film gets more credit than it deserves, and his performance doesn't change that for me. He's fine in the few things I've seen. Just fine. I haven't even seen Creed.

![My Unpopular Actor Opinions](/images/posts/my-unpopular-actor-opinions/image-5.jpg)

**Channing Tatum:** He doesn't need explaining. I don't care, I never have, and I don't expect that to change.

![My Unpopular Actor Opinions](/images/posts/my-unpopular-actor-opinions/image-6.jpg)

**Christian Bale:** The hardest one to justify, because he's objectively one of the best actors working right now. The transformations, the accents, the way he vanishes into a role instead of just playing it, all real, all deserved. I'm just not a fan.

![My Unpopular Actor Opinions](/images/posts/my-unpopular-actor-opinions/image-7.jpg)

**Terrence Howard:** This one's more personal. It's his ***voice***. Something about it makes me tune out the second he's on screen. There's one silver lining: he played James Rhodey Rhodes in the first Iron Man and was supposed to return for the sequel, but that reportedly fell through over a pay dispute with the studio, and Don Cheadle took over. Both have said it's water under the bridge now. I'm relieved, because Iron Man 2 with the original casting would've been rough for me to sit through.

## The Women

![My Unpopular Actor Opinions](/images/posts/my-unpopular-actor-opinions/image-8.jpg)

**Gal Gadot:** She tests my patience the most, because the acting just isn't good. She seems warm and likable in interviews, which almost makes it worse, since people extend her a grace the performances don't deserve. The first Wonder Woman got away with a lot because the character was a fish out of water by design, so playing stiff and unsure worked in her favor. The sequel removed that safety net, and suddenly the limitations were impossible to ignore. I think a lot of people who fell for the first movie quietly noticed during the second, and that correction was way overdue.

![My Unpopular Actor Opinions](/images/posts/my-unpopular-actor-opinions/image-9.jpg)

**Ruby Rose:** She's built a whole career around one expression, and it shows up whether the scene calls for it or not. She broke out on **Orange Is the New Black**, moved into action roles like Resident Evil, xXx, and John Wick: Chapter 2, and landed the lead on Batwoman. She just tries too hard, if you ask me.

![My Unpopular Actor Opinions](/images/posts/my-unpopular-actor-opinions/image-10.jpg)

**Gwyneth Paltrow:** This one's hard to put a finger on. It's not one bad role I can point to, it's her general presence. Her voice, her eyes, the way she carries herself on camera, something about her always seems *slightly removed* from everyone else in the scene, like she's not quite from around ***here***. I can't prove it yet. Still working on that theory.

![My Unpopular Actor Opinions](/images/posts/my-unpopular-actor-opinions/image-11.jpg)

**Joey King:** Probably the least fair entry on here, because I don't think this is about her acting at all. It's her face. Something about it just doesn't work for me on screen, even though she seems perfectly likable whenever I catch her in interviews. This one probably says more about me being petty than about her performances. I'll take that on the chin.

---

No hate, no malice, just a simple list. These are the actors I've never been able to get into, and I'm not sure I ever will.
