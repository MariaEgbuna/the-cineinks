Centered on the takeover of Las Encinas by a powerful alumni syndicate, *Elite* Season 8 brings the show's eight-season run to a close through a web of blackmail, sex work rings, and murder. The final season traces the investigation into Joel's death before culminating in the exposure of systemic corruption and the school's shutdown.

*This post contains spoilers for Season 8 of Netflix's Elite.*

*Content note: this recap mentions sexual assault, coercion, murder, and drug overdoses.*

### The Quick Version (TL;DR)
* **The Takeover:** Wealthy siblings Héctor and Emilia Krawietz turn the school's Alumni association into an abusive, cult-like syndicate, pulling Joel and Chloe into dangerous initiation trials.
* **The Murder:** On graduation night, Joel is found with his throat slit inside the Alumni club, leading to the arrests of Iván and Dalmar while multiple suspects point fingers at one another.
* **The Finale:** Hidden phone footage proves corrupt detective Luis killed Joel. Omar exposes eight seasons of cover-ups on a livestream, Isadora fatally poisons Luis during his arrest, and Las Encinas shuts down for good.

---

## The Krawietz Siblings Take Over

The final season opens on graduation night with two partygoers finding Joel's dead body before rewinding to the start of the semester. Omar reunites with Nadia during her visit home from New York, while wealthy siblings Héctor and Emilia Krawietz seize control of the exclusive Alumni club at Las Encinas.

Desperate for money and status, several students get pulled into the Krawietz siblings' orbit:
* **Joel & Iván:** After Iván returns from South Africa, a bitter argument pushes Joel to accept Héctor's sponsorship into Alumni. To stay in the club, Joel endures degrading trials, secret sex work arrangements orchestrated by Emilia, and coercive encounters with Héctor, all while quietly using the cash to help Nico's family and Dalmar.
* **Chloe & Carmen:** Low on funds and worried about police investigating Raúl's death, Carmen pushes Chloe to win Emilia's favor. Emilia forces Chloe to sabotage Eric's bipolar medication, leading Chloe to break up with Eric and betray Sonia and Dalmar by leaking compromising photos and videos of them.
* **Isadora & Luis:** Facing financial ruin after loan shark Cibrán has her father killed in prison, Isadora loses control of her club to Iván (who rebrands it Cruz9) while corrupt detective Luis extorts and assaults her over her club's permits.

Meanwhile, Dalmar faces deportation and tries to blackmail Carmen using his video of her pushing Raúl off the roof. When Carmen outsmarts him, Joel steps in and blackmails Carmen himself for 250,000 euros, prompting Chloe and Eric to hatch a plan to scare Joel off.

## The Night Joel Dies and the Wrong Suspects

Ahead of an Alumni orgy scheduled on the same night as the seniors' graduation keg party, Héctor's gang jumps Omar and leaves him in a coma, threatening Nadia's UN internship if he speaks up. At the orgy, Chloe sneaks Eric inside to confront Joel with a knife. Joel fights back and Dalmar intervenes, causing Chloe and Eric to flee and leave Eric's knife behind.

Later that night, Joel sends Iván a breakup text and sleeps with Héctor one last time in exchange for Héctor using his connections to protect Isadora from Luis. Outside, someone knocks Dalmar unconscious while he waits with a drunk Iván. Inside, after Héctor falls asleep, someone slits Joel's throat with Eric's forgotten knife.

Police arrest Iván and Dalmar at the scene, and suspicion quickly spreads across the group:
* **Dalmar Framed:** Exploiting racist backlash online, the Alumni members paint Dalmar as the killer, and Carmen pressures him into signing a false confession in exchange for supporting his family.
* **The Krawietz Fallout:** Sonia uncovers drone footage showing two figures moving a bag near the club. Héctor accuses Emilia of killing Joel out of jealousy, and Emilia admits to drugging Iván, texting from Joel's phone to frame him, and knocking out Dalmar, though she insists Héctor committed the actual murder. Both siblings are arrested.
* **The Hidden Footage:** Chloe realizes she left her phone recording inside the Alumni room the night of the party. Iván, Isadora, and Sara team up to steal the phone back from Chloe, unlocking the video that reveals the real killer.

## Luis and the Livestream

The footage reveals that Detective Luis entered the Alumni room, picked up Eric's forgotten knife, and murdered Joel to silence him after Joel threatened to expose Luis's extortion of Isadora.

When Omar tries to take the evidence to the police, Luis intercepts and attacks him before Detective Luena interrupts. Realizing the system will never hold anyone accountable, Nadia encourages Omar to tear out the root of the problem. Omar goes live on social media with Sara, publicly exposing eight seasons of covered-up crimes from Marina and Samuel's deaths to Joel's murder, before broadcasting the video of Luis killing Joel.

At the same time, Isadora lures Luis to her suite to poison him. Police storm in after seeing Omar's livestream and arrest Luis, but Isadora slips a fatal dose of drugs into him as he is led away in handcuffs, causing him to collapse and die from a seizure.

With Joel's murder solved, Dalmar is freed and hands the video of Raúl's death directly to Sara, leaving it up to her to pursue justice. Sara warns Eric she is coming after Carmen and Chloe, prompting Carmen and Chloe to flee Madrid after Chloe says a tearful goodbye to Eric. Following Omar's livestream, outraged parents protest outside Las Encinas, forcing Principal Virginia to shut the school down permanently. In the final scene, the remaining seniors walk into a public high school to finish their final exams.

## How the Show Ended

* **Joel:** Murdered on graduation night by Luis using Eric's forgotten knife.
* **Omar & Nadia:** Omar exposes eight seasons of Las Encinas cover-ups on a livestream with Sara, and Nadia stays in Madrid to support him.
* **Iván:** Cleared of Joel's murder and finishes his final exams at a public school.
* **Dalmar:** Cleared and released from custody, handing the footage of Raúl's murder over to Sara.
* **Chloe & Carmen:** Flee Madrid together after Dalmar turns the roof footage over to Sara.
* **Eric & Nico:** Reconcile and transfer to the public school alongside their classmates.
* **Isadora:** Retains ownership of Cruz9 and fatally poisons Luis during his arrest.
* **Sara:** Exposes Joel's killer alongside Omar, reconciles with Nico, and holds the evidence to take down Carmen.
* **Héctor & Emilia Krawietz:** Released after Luis is exposed, leaving Madrid together on cold terms.
* **Luis:** Exposed on Omar's livestream and dies from a seizure after Isadora poisons him during his arrest.
* **Las Encinas:** Permanently shut down following public outrage over the school's corruption.
