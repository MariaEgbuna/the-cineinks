Centered on the takeover of Las Encinas by a powerful alumni syndicate, Elite Season 8 brings the show's eight-season run to a close through a web of blackmail, sex work rings, and murder. The final season traces the investigation into Joel's death before culminating in the exposure of systemic corruption and the school's shutdown.

_This post contains spoilers for Season 8 of Netflix's Elite._

_Content note: this recap mentions sexual assault, coercion, murder, and drug overdoses._

---

### The Krawietz siblings take over

The season opens on graduation night, with two drunk girls stumbling across Joel's dead body. Then it rewinds.

In the present, Omar's reunited with Nadia, who's home for a short stay. At Las Encinas, siblings Hector and Emilia Krawietz have taken over the Alumni club, to Principal Virginia's displeasure, though there's little she can do about one of the wealthiest families in Spain. Alumni's now exclusive, with sponsorship required to join, and the connections are real: Nadia credits her UN internship to them.

Hector's drawn to Joel and hits on him despite Joel's disinterest. Joel's still trying to reach Ivan, who's ignoring his calls from South Africa. Chloe and Eric are happy together, which needles Nico, and Sonia's now with Dalmar. Sara publicly accuses Chloe of killing Raúl, and she's not far off: the actual killer, Chloe's mother Carmen, is a real suspect. With no money for a lawyer, Carmen pushes Chloe toward Hector's wealth. Isa's in a financial bind too, and her mother nudges her toward the Krawietzs. She's hired Omar to rebuild the club's image and attract investors while dodging Detective Luis, who keeps pushing for a friendship she doesn't want.

Dalmar's facing deportation, though the notice has a loophole: cooperate with the police or government and documentation becomes possible. He's sitting on a recording of Carmen pushing Raúl off the rooftop.

Omar comes home to find Joel crashing in his room and throws a fit, given Joel's history of cheating on him with Ivan. Joel lies about staying at Ivan's and sneaks off to the Alumni club, where Hector finds him and offers to sponsor him. Joel isn't buying it.

Chloe and Isa both angle for Emilia's sponsorship, and Isa has the stronger pitch, offering her club to Alumni. Nico blames Chloe for being a bad influence on Eric. Trying to cheer Chloe up, Sonia reveals that Isa's broke, a secret that traveled from Omar to Dalmar to her. Chloe leaks Isa's finances to Emilia and wins the sponsorship. Emilia toys with her the whole time.

Ivan returns to Spain, and Joel brushes off Omar's check-in. Ivan and Joel's reunion sours because Ivan's still stinging over Joel not joining him abroad. Joel calls him entitled and accepts Hector's invitation out of spite. Emilia warns her brother that every Alumni candidate has to pass a test that pushes them past their limits, and Hector agrees to it.

The episode ends back at graduation, with a bloodied Ivan and Dalmar arrested for Joel's murder.

### Joel pays his way in

Ivan and Joel wrestle in gym class while Emilia teases her brother for being jealous. A flash-forward shows Hector waking up next to Joel's body, his throat slit.

In the present, Carmen tells a cornered Chloe she'll handle the police. Emilia's first test for Chloe is to swap Eric's BPD medication for drugs, sending him into a panic everyone mistakes for a breakdown. Chloe stays to care for him, and Emilia says her priorities disqualify her. Isa has Omar running errands nonstop, and Nadia tells him to stand up for himself. Luis overhears Isa and Omar planning a party and worrying about money.

Joel's first trial is Hector telling him to secretly record girls in the locker room shower. Principal Virginia catches him, and Hector saves him by threatening to leak footage if she expels him, then coerces Joel into a sexual act and calls it part of the trial. Elsewhere, Nico helps Sara see she's been romanticizing an abusive relationship by taking her to Raúl's old place.

Joel now bartends at Isa's club. Dalmar nudges him to give Ivan a chance, and Joel blows Hector off, until Ivan pays Isa to let him take Joel home. Joel's humiliated. Eric shows up on the dance floor insisting he's fine, but the group blames Chloe for his episode anyway. She breaks down, ends things with Eric by voice message, and tells Emilia she's ready to join Alumni. In the chaos, Luis has someone sabotage the party decor, injuring several people including Joel, and uses the liability to extort Isa over her permits. Omar has a panic attack, and Nadia, who was about to leave, decides to stay and look after him.

Dalmar's bike is stolen, and reporting it gets him arrested for lacking ID. Sonia bails him out. With his citizenship on the line, he anonymously blackmails Carmen with the footage, demanding the two million Ivan gave her. Joel goes to Hector and agrees to do whatever it takes to join Alumni, and they hook up.

By episode three, Chloe and Joel are both accepted into Alumni. A flash-forward shows Joel apologizing to Isa at graduation while she warns him he'll regret whatever he's done. In the present, Luis forces Isa into dinner in exchange for a visit with her imprisoned father. Hector demands a two thousand euro fee from Joel to stay in the club, and Joel won't let Hector cover it. Hector rigs a race he always wins so Joel can bet on him and pay Ivan back. Omar warns him it sounds like a cult, and Joel says he can handle it.

Dalmar's extortion backfires. Carmen hands over a bag mostly full of paper, tracks him home with a hidden device, and threatens to frame him unless he returns the money, though pity gets him a spare thousand. His deportation notice shrinks to one month. Isa's visit with her father goes just as badly: Martin insists she sell the club to a loan shark named Cibran to clear his debts. She relapses into drugs. Omar suggests borrowing from Ivan instead, but his doubt that they can save the club offends her. Roberta forces her to approach Cibran anyway, and his offer doesn't cover the debt.

At school, Isa overhears Joel and Nico discussing the Alumni race and bets her own club on it. Chloe tells Ivan, who arrives in time to catch Joel and Hector making out but can't stop Isa from entering. Mid-race, Isa crashes and Ivan rushes to her, while Joel, heading toward Hector, is struck by Emilia's car. He calls out for Ivan as Hector and Chloe pull him away. Emilia wins and tells Isa the club's hers by forfeit, until Ivan reveals he bought it the night before. Emilia warns that Isa still owes her. That same day, Isa learns Cibran killed her father, and a devastated Roberta blames her. Luis comforts a shocked Isa. Joel confronts Luis, threatens to expose him, then drops a beer bottle on his foot and walks off.

Episode four opens with a wealthy Alumni couple, Pier and Guillermina, offering a huge donation in exchange for time with Joel. Emilia's thrilled, and Hector flatly refuses. Isa keeps apologizing to Ivan, who promises to help with the club's reopening. When Ivan asks Joel directly if he's with Hector, Hector interrupts by inviting Joel over, and Ivan warns him and walks away. Chloe tries to reconcile with a heartbroken Eric, who swears to cut her off. Emilia overhears and invites him to a pool party, where Joel and Hector hook up at one end of the pool while Emilia and Eric do the same at the other, the siblings watching each other the whole time.

Carmen and Chloe plan to isolate Dalmar and then swoop in as his saviors so he won't leak the Raúl footage, starting by getting him fired. Chloe sets Sonia up with a guy and secretly photographs it. Sara invites Nico over, and after they sleep together while she's high, she spirals into believing he took advantage of her and kicks him out. Back at the club, Eric grows friendly with Chloe again now that he's moved on with Emilia. Furious, Chloe leaks Sonia's photo online, then seduces and secretly films Dalmar and posts that too. Sonia sees it and is devastated.

Emilia, jealous of her brother's obsession with Joel, secretly arranges the meeting with Pier and Guillermina. Hector rushes to stop it and clashes with a clueless Omar, who throws him out. Joel refuses the couple until the amount of money changes his mind. A flash-forward shows the resulting orgy, with Joel occupied while Chloe pulls Eric into another room, where he produces a knife. In the present, a group of Alumni members led by Hector jumps Omar and leaves him bleeding in an alley.

### The night Joel dies

Episode five opens with Omar in a coma. Nico's father tells the family he needs specialized care they can't afford, until Joel quietly pays for it and covers Dalmar's rent. Ivan takes over Isadora House and rebrands it Cruz9. He and Joel reconcile and talk about moving in together ahead of a keg party celebrating their early graduation. But the Alumni schedule an orgy for the same night and name Joel the planner. Hector, furious, threatens to tell Ivan himself, and Joel beats him to it. Ivan's upset to learn how Joel's been earning money.

The group freezes Chloe out over what she did to Sonia, until Eric reconciles with her, which upsets Nico further. Dalmar learns Carmen plans to buy his silence by supporting his family, and reluctantly agrees to work the orgy for the pay. Behind his back, Joel blackmails Carmen for a quarter million euros, giving her two days before he leaks the footage. Chloe panics and tells Eric, who offers to help scare Joel into backing down.

At the club's reopening, Isa helps broker a conversation between Ivan and Joel, who admits he needs independence but wants Ivan's love and promises the blackmail is temporary. At the hospital, Omar wakes to Alumni members threatening Nadia's internship if he reports his assault. Luis gathers evidence of hookups at Isa's club to threaten her with running an unlicensed brothel, and when she refuses his deal, he assaults her. Isa turns to Joel, who apologizes and confronts Luis, threatening exposure.

At the orgy, Chloe sneaks Eric in and corners Joel alone, and Eric pulls the knife on him. Joel fights back, and Dalmar, hearing the struggle, intervenes. Chloe and Eric flee, and Eric drops the knife. At the keg party, Sara opens up to Nico about not being able to process feelings for anyone but Raúl and apologizes for her accusation. Nico forgives her, right before she notices Carmen, Chloe, and Eric acting suspiciously and follows them. Hector gets Joel to sleep with him one more time in exchange for using his connections to protect Isa from Luis. Outside, a devastated Ivan gets a breakup text from Joel and gets drunk, and someone knocks Dalmar out while he waits with him. Inside, after Hector falls asleep, Joel gets up to send Ivan one last message when someone slits his throat.

### The wrong suspects

Episode six picks up with Omar, healed and home, texting a Joel who's already dead. Emilia comforts a distraught Hector, who has no memory of the night and is convinced he killed Joel out of jealousy. Police detain the group. Suspicion falls on Eric once his knife turns up as the murder weapon, and a panicked Nico backtracks and blames Chloe for manipulating his cousin, which wounds Eric further. Under formal questioning, Dalmar mentions the orgy and Ivan corroborates it. Omar confronts Emilia, who deflects everything onto Dalmar and dismisses Joel and the others as sex workers, a lie Omar seems to believe in his shock.

The group rallies around Ivan's popularity to clear his name. Chloe searches for her missing phone, unaware it's still hidden in the Alumni room, where she left it planning to blackmail Joel herself. The Alumni paint Dalmar as the culprit using racial framing, and Omar's fury deepens when Principal Virginia stays silent. Carmen offers to bail Ivan out but says she can't do the same for Dalmar. She pressures him to sign a false confession in exchange for supporting his family, using the wave of racist online hate as leverage. Dalmar refuses and tells police about Joel's blackmail of Carmen as the real motive, though Detective Luena needs a corroborating witness before she can act.

Sonia recalls drone footage from the keg party and tracks down its owner, finding two figures near the Alumni club carrying a large bag the night of the murder. Hector starts to suspect his sister framed him out of jealousy over Joel. Omar and Sonia take the footage to the police, convinced it shows Hector and Emilia moving Joel's body, but the police reveal that Dalmar has already confessed.

In episode seven, Omar and Sonia visit Dalmar to ask why he took the fall. He explains the hate campaign and Carmen's deal, and tells them what he knows about Raúl's murder. He's convinced Carmen killed Joel to protect that secret, not Eric. Theories split: Omar and Sonia suspect the Krawietz siblings, while Ivan and Isa still suspect Chloe and Eric. Nadia learns her father's health is failing and gives up her flight to New York to stay. Omar admits the real reason he stayed silent about his assault, the Alumni's threat against her internship. She says she doesn't care about losing it, and they agree to report the Krawietzs together.

At school, Sara and Nico fight over her suspicion that Chloe manipulated Eric into murder. Eric overhears and worries Nico told her the truth, and Nico denies it and turns the blame back on Eric for not trusting him. Sara eavesdrops on Isa and Ivan working through their theory about Chloe and Eric and fills in gaps for them without them knowing where it's coming from. Chloe finds her dead phone in the Alumni room but can't see what it recorded until it charges.

Hector, furious with his sister, keeps his distance, and they arrive separately to an Alumni party at Cruz9. Ivan, Sara, and Isa use the party to steal Chloe's phone: Ivan distracts her with a selfie, Sara sends her off to check on Eric, and Ivan grabs the phone and rushes to Isa's suite. Upstairs, Omar tips off Luis and Luena, who confront Hector and Emilia together. Hector accuses his sister of killing Joel, and she admits everything, shown in flashback: cleaning up the crime scene, drugging Ivan, texting from Joel's phone to frame him, and knocking out Dalmar when he unexpectedly tagged along. Facing arrest, she insists she's only an accomplice and that Hector's the real killer, driven by jealousy over Joel and Ivan. Both siblings are taken into custody, and Emilia coldly reminds her brother he owes her now.

Chloe finds Eric safe, realizes her phone's missing and that Ivan tricked her, and rushes with Eric to the suite, where Ivan and Isa block their way. Behind them, Sara's found the real footage from the night of the orgy, and together they see who actually killed Joel.

---

### Luis and the livestream

The finale shows the truth in full: Luis picks up Eric's forgotten knife and slits Joel's throat, all of it caught on Chloe's phone. In the present, Omar's already working an angle to bring down both Luis and the Krawietzs at once.

He first offers Hector and Emilia the real footage in exchange for a signed confession admitting they assaulted him and left him for dead. Emilia doesn't believe he has the video. Hector counters by promising to shut the Alumni club down completely if Omar hands it over. Omar hesitates, since that would let Hector walk away free, and Hector doesn't wait for an answer. He goes straight to Luis and tells him Omar has the footage. Luis tears Omar's apartment apart looking for it, and a shaken Nadia tells her brother to stop playing games with people this dangerous and go to the police.

Omar heads to the station, but Luis intercepts him before he can reach Luena and tries to assault him, only stopping when Luena walks in. Omar flees and tells the others what happened. Furious, Isa decides to take matters into her own hands. She invites Luis over that night, planning to seduce and kill him herself.

Ivan objects, so Isa drugs him too, just enough to keep him from stopping her. Meanwhile, Nadia plants a different idea in Omar's head: maybe the only real solution is tearing out the root of the problem. Omar pulls Sara in and asks her to go live with him. On camera, he lays out everything Las Encinas has buried over the years, from Samuel and Marina's deaths to the accidents that nearly killed him and Ivan, and says nobody's ever been held accountable. Then he has Sara upload the footage of Luis killing Joel, live, for everyone to see.

At the same time, Isa's upstairs flirting with Luis, waiting to inject him with drugs, but he catches on and a struggle breaks out. Luena, having just watched the livestream, arrives to find a drugged Ivan trying desperately to reach Isa. Police storm in and arrest Luis before he can seriously hurt her. As Ivan and Omar help her up, Isa tells Luis what she thinks of him: his jealousy over lives he could never have drove him to force his way into theirs by violence. Then, as he's led out in handcuffs, she quietly injects him one more time. He seizes while trying to call for help and dies before anyone can reach him.

Hector and Emilia are released and decide to leave together, with Hector keeping a cold distance from his sister the whole way out.

With Joel's case resolved, Dalmar's set free. Carmen, terrified he'll still release the footage of her killing Raúl, plans to flee the country with Chloe, though Chloe wants to stay. She makes one last attempt to buy Dalmar's silence, offering a hundred thousand euros and legal help with his citizenship in exchange for deleting the video. Sara tries livestreaming the last days of high school but can't get past her grief over Raúl, until Nico reminds her he was abusive and got what he deserved, and she livestreams everyone else preparing to leave instead. Nico reconciles with Eric, admitting he loves him despite everything between them, while a still-drugged Ivan rambles emotionally about how precious life is.

Dalmar hands the footage of Raúl's murder directly to Sara, telling her it's her call and that he doesn't care if it costs him his citizenship. Sara warns Eric she's coming for Carmen and Chloe no matter what. Eric rushes to stop Chloe from leaving and offers to go with her, but Carmen tells him he belongs with his own family and that if it's meant to happen, he and Chloe will find their way back to each other. After a quiet goodbye, Chloe and Carmen leave together.

After Omar's livestream, worried parents protest outside the school, and Principal Virginia makes the call to shut Las Encinas down for good. The graduating seniors finish their final exams at a public school instead, and the series ends on Ivan, Isa, Eric, Sonia, Nico, and Sara walking through its doors, overdressed and out of place, while the other students stare.

---

### How the show ended

* **Joel:** Dead. Luis killed him in the Alumni room on graduation night with Eric's forgotten knife.
* **Omar:** Alive. He goes live with Sara and exposes eight seasons' worth of covered-up crimes. Nadia chooses to stay.
* **Ivan:** Cleared of Joel's murder.
* **Dalmar:** Released from custody once Luis is unmasked.
* **Chloe:** Leaves Madrid with Carmen after Sara says she's coming for them.
* **Eric:** Stays behind and reconciles with Nico. Heads to the public school with the rest of the group.
* **Isa:** Alive. She injects Luis as he's led away by police. Cruz9 is still hers.
* **Nico and Sara:** They find their way to each other.
* **Hector and Emilia Krawietz:** Arrested, then released. They leave together.
* **Luis:** Dead. Isa injected him mid-arrest, and he died of a seizure.
* **Las Encinas:** Shut down permanently.
