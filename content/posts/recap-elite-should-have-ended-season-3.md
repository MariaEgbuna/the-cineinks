Season 3 brings the original three-season mystery of *Elite* to a climax as Polo returns to school on bail, police interrogations frame a graduation night tragedy, and hidden alliances collapse under the weight of Marina's unsolved murder.

### The Quick Version (TL;DR)
* **The Setup:** Polo returns to Las Encinas while Carla is blackmailed into retracting her statement, and Polo's mothers attempt to smooth things over with a scholarship.
* **The Fallout:** Ander faces secret leukemia treatments, Samuel's informant deal backfires on Rebeka's family, and romantic betrayals explode at a blackout party.
* **The Finale:** Polo is accidentally stabbed with a broken champagne bottle by Lu during a graduation party confrontation, and his classmates stage the scene as a suicide.

---

## Polo Returns and Secrets Fester

![RECAP: Elite Season 3](/images/posts/recap-elite-season-3/elite-s3.jpg)

Polo returns to school on bail after Carla retracts her confession due to family pressures, prompting Guzman to abduct Polo in a desperate bid to force a confession. Personal chaos spreads across the student body:
* **Lu & Valerio:** Cut off by their father, they scramble for a college scholarship funded by Polo's mothers.
* **Samuel & Rebeka:** Samuel trades police cooperation against Rebeka's mother to clear Nano, only to be exposed by UV paint at a blackout party. Nadia discovers Omar is having an affair with Malick.
* **Ander & Carla:** Ander hides his leukemia diagnosis to push Omar away, while Carla is forced by her father to date Yeray, nearly drowning before Polo rescues her. Polo also enters a brief polyamorous relationship with Cayetana and Valerio.

## Graduation Night

![RECAP: Elite Season 3](/images/posts/recap-elite-season-3/lu-and-nadia.jpg)

Expulsions and ruined futures peak at graduation. Lu uses their shared scholarship acceptance speech to blast Polo's family, causing the funding to be yanked. After Polo finally confesses to his parents over dinner, he confronts Cayetana at the graduation party for sabotaging his plans to leave the country. When Lu throws a drink in Polo's face and shatters a champagne bottle, an argument in the bathroom turns fatal as Lu stabs him in the chest. Polo stumbles out and falls through a glass railing to the dance floor, forgiven by Guzman in his final moments while Carla pockets the broken bottle neck.

## The Cover-Up and a New Year

![RECAP: Elite Season 3](/images/posts/recap-elite-season-3/polo.jpg)

The group passes the bottle neck around to pool their fingerprints and stages a suicide. During the police investigation, Guzman sets up a circular alibi timeline. Ultimately, Polo's parents end the inquiry themselves by turning in the real murder weapon, the trophy, and declaring that Polo confessed to Marina's death before taking his own life.

---

## Where We Left Everyone

* **Polo:** Dead, with his parents closing the case to protect the family name.
* **Lu & Nadia:** Leaving for college in New York together, backed by Nadia's family.
* **Carla:** Departing to study abroad after handing her family winery to Valerio.
* **Omar & Ander:** Reconciled in Madrid after discovering Ander's cancer is in remission.
* **Guzman, Samuel & Rebeka:** Repeating their final year at Las Encinas.
* **Cayetana:** Working as the school janitor.
