With Polo back at school on bail and Marina's murder still unresolved, Elite Season 3 pushes every rivalry and secret toward a breaking point during the students' final semester. Framed around police interrogations after a body falls through the glass at a graduation club, the eight-episode season brings the show's original three-season mystery to a close.

_This post contains spoilers for Season 3 of Netflix's Elite._

---

## A body on the dance floor

![RECAP: Elite Season 3](/images/posts/recap-elite-season-3/elite-s3.jpg)

The season opens at a graduation party gone wrong: a body crashes down through the club's upper level onto the dance floor, and Carla stands nearby, stunned, next to shattered glass. Then it rewinds five months.

Polo's out on bail, but the case isn't closed. A judge orders a formal confrontation between him and Carla to compare their accounts. Carla tells him she's done covering for him, though she'll give him a chance to confess first. Her father has other plans: he blackmails her into staying silent, warning that the truth could expose his own dealings and take her mother's business down with it.

A new face shows up at Nadia's family store: Malick, another Muslim student at Las Encinas, and there's some chemistry. A postcard from Nano finally arrives from Asilah in Morocco. He's healthy and settling in, though he doesn't sound convinced Polo will ever face justice.

Ander finds a growth that worries him enough that Omar pushes him to see a doctor. Valerio shows up at school strung out on cocaine and living out of his car after being disowned. Lu invites him to stay while their father's away, but their father comes home early and catches them together. Lu insists nothing forced ever happened and that she chose it. He tells her that if she wants to live like an adult, she'll be cut off financially and thrown out the moment she turns eighteen.

Samuel makes one more attempt to reach Carla, telling her he loves her no matter what she decides about testifying. She slaps him, accusing him of only loving her on the condition that she does what he wants.

At the college fair, the pressure keeps stacking. Polo's unraveling, calling Ander over and over only to be ignored. Cayetana still won't get rid of the murder weapon, saying it's safer in her closet. A university recruiter tells Nadia that the rumors from Lu's leaked video are enough to cost her a scholarship. Ander gets his test results back and breaks down in front of Rebeka. And when Guzman spots Polo flipping through an Oxford brochure, the same school that was Marina's dream, he attacks Polo on the spot, hard enough that the principal threatens expulsion for anyone who steps out of line again.

At the confrontation, Polo looks like he's about to break and admit everything, until Carla cuts in first and claims the whole accusation was a lie. Guzman turns on Samuel for it, convinced Carla just proved she was never trustworthy. Samuel's resolve to get Polo caught only hardens, and he tells his own mother to leave for Morocco and be with Nano, choosing to stay behind alone.

Then the timeline snaps back to the dance floor, and a detail nobody saw coming: someone stabbed him before he ever hit the ground. Carla tells the Inspector she ran straight down after the fall, but she actually followed a blood trail to the bathroom first and pocketed a broken, bloody bottle. When the Inspector searches her bag, it's gone. Carla insists, shaken, that she could never have hurt him, she loved him. Because the body on the floor is Polo.

## Polo confesses

![RECAP: Elite Season 3](/images/posts/recap-elite-season-3/lu-and-nadia.jpg)

Nadia has real motivation to win the scholarship this time, especially once it comes out that the funding is coming from Polo's mothers, basically a bribe dressed up as opportunity for anyone connected to the case. Lu is chasing it too, cut off financially by her father. Nadia tries to pressure her out of the running by threatening to expose her and Valerio, but Lu calls the bluff, and Nadia backs off once she learns just how broke Lu is.

Guzman starts playing a longer game with Polo, acting friendlier and letting him believe he's been forgiven. He lures Polo to his house, drugs him, ties him up, and plays Marina's old dance footage on a loop while demanding to know where the trophy is. Polo finally cracks and admits he killed her, insists it was an accident, but won't give up the trophy's location. Guzman nearly strangles him before Samuel steps in, and what's left is a Guzman who's scared of what he's capable of.

## Secrets start to cost everyone

Samuel's situation gets messier by the episode. His mom leaves to join Nano in Morocco, leaving him alone with rent he can't cover, and Valerio moves in once he cleans himself up. Samuel also strikes a deal with the police: help take down Rebeka's mother's drug operation in exchange for progress clearing Nano's name. He agrees, not realizing how badly it'll complicate things with Rebeka, who he's also started something real with.

Samuel keeps feeding information to the police while Rebeka has no idea he's the reason her mother ends up arrested. Rebeka and Valerio start dealing again out of financial desperation, and it all comes crashing down at a blackout party, where UV paint exposes Samuel's hands as the one who stole evidence meant to identify who tipped off the police. In the same reveal, Omar and Malick's secret affair comes out, and Nadia sees them. Rebeka ends things with Samuel on the spot. Nadia slaps Malick and walks out.

Ander's world changes fast. What starts as a worrying growth turns into a leukemia diagnosis, and he spends weeks hiding it before telling his mother and Omar. He tries to push Omar away, insisting Omar deserves better than watching him get sick, but Omar refuses to leave. As the treatment starts failing to respond, Ander isolates himself and eventually lies to Omar about being with someone else just to force him out the door.

Someone starts impersonating Polo online, tweeting confessions about Marina's murder and taunting details of her final moments, which throws suspicion on Samuel and Guzman. Neither confirms or denies it. It later comes out that at least one of those tweets, the cruelest one, mocking Polo's suicide attempt, came from Cayetana, though nobody in the group finds out for a long time.

Pushed by her father to stay attached to Yeray for the family's financial survival, Carla starts using heavily to cope and nearly drowns in a pool mid-party after taking too much, saved only because Polo happens to notice in time. Rebeka sold her the drugs, and the guilt is enough to push Rebeka to finally quit dealing, at least for a while.

Polo, Cayetana, and Valerio end up in a relationship together, which briefly seems to work until Polo's parents catch them and make their disgust with Valerio impossible to ignore, quietly torching Valerio's chances at studying abroad with Polo's help.

Nadia's father essentially forces her into an engagement with Malick to keep the peace, not knowing about the affair. She finds her own way out, choosing to go to New York alone rather than live a lie, right after a raw monologue to her parents about no longer being the daughter they think she is.

By the time graduation approaches, nearly everyone's paying for something. Samuel, Guzman, Valerio, and Rebeka all get expelled over the drug fallout, and the group splinters over who's to blame, with Polo suspected but never confirmed as the one who reported it. Guzman admits to Samuel that he's done hating Polo, since it hasn't changed anything. Ander tells his mother he doesn't have long left and refuses to spend what time remains studying for exams he may not live to take.

## Graduation night

At the graduation ceremony, everything that's been simmering boils over. Yeray forces Teo's hand and installs Carla in charge of the family business as a condition of funding, finally giving her some real power. Polo ends things with Cayetana for good, telling her she never loved him, just the life he could give her. Lu and Nadia win the scholarship together on stage, only for Lu to go off script mid-speech and tear into Polo and his family in front of the entire school, which gets the scholarship pulled by his humiliated mother. Samuel learns the deal he made with the police was a setup from the start, and that giving up Rebeka's mother's location got Nano arrested rather than cleared. That night, over dinner, Polo finally tells his parents the truth: he killed Marina, and he wants it made right by getting Nadia and Lu's scholarship reinstated.

The graduation party that follows brings a strange peace to a few corners of the group: Guzman and Nadia find their way back to each other for whatever time they have left, and Ander and Omar share one last real goodbye before Omar leaves for New York with Nadia and Malick. It's the last calm moment before the body hits the dance floor.

![RECAP: Elite Season 3](/images/posts/recap-elite-season-3/polo.jpg)

Polo shows up to graduation for one reason: he wants the trophy from Cayetana so he can turn himself in. She refuses, and when he asks if she's the one who sabotaged his chance to transfer abroad, she admits it, saying she loves him and couldn't let him go. Before he can process that, Lu storms up drunk and throws a drink in his face, blaming him for wrecking her life. He tries to tell her the scholarship's being reinstated but she's past listening.

The night keeps sliding sideways. Omar refuses to even hand Polo a napkin at the bar. Samuel, drunk and spiraling, spots a knife and moves toward Polo with real intent before Guzman physically pulls him off. Lu grabs a champagne bottle from Valerio and jokes to Nadia about smashing it over Polo's head. Samuel even asks Rebeka to have someone rough Polo up, and she says no.

Guzman intercepts Polo near Lu and Nadia and tells him to leave before he gets hurt worse than last time. Polo asks, one more time, if Guzman could ever forgive him once he confesses. Guzman says he never will. In the middle of that exchange, Lu drops her champagne bottle and it shatters, and the broken neck of it gets kicked out of sight across the floor.

Lu follows Polo into the bathroom holding that broken bottle neck. She unloads on him for everything he's taken from her and Guzman by killing Marina. Polo lashes back with something cruel, echoing almost exactly what he once said to Marina right before he killed her. Lu snaps, pins him against the wall, and the bottle goes into his chest. She's horrified the second it happens, pulling it out and begging him to know she didn't mean it. He tells her he knows, but it doesn't stop her from falling apart.

Polo stumbles out bleeding, trying to reach Guzman, but slips and hits a cracked glass barrier that gives way under him, and he falls to the dance floor below. Guzman sprints down after him, and Carla, seeing the whole thing, quietly pockets the bottle neck to protect Lu. By the time Guzman reaches him, there's nothing left to do but hold his hand. He tells Polo he forgives him, the one thing Polo begged for all night, and Polo dies holding onto that.

## A cover-up and a new year

The group closes ranks. Carla pulls Samuel aside and tells him the truth: Lu did it, by accident. Valerio points out that everyone already saw her throw a drink at Polo, so no one will believe an accident story. So they cover it up together: passing the bottle neck around so everyone's fingerprints are on it, staging the scene to look like suicide, and letting Cayetana, playing grieving girlfriend, press Polo's fingers to the weapon herself. She cuts her own hand doing it and doesn't say a word before tossing the bottle onto the dance floor for the police to find.

When the police start questioning everyone, Guzman comes up with the plan that saves them: everyone says they saw a different person follow Polo into the bathroom. Samuel names Valerio. Valerio names Cayetana. Cayetana names Ander. Ander names Guzman. Guzman names Omar. Omar names his own sister. Nadia names Carla. Carla names Rebeka. Rebeka names Lu. Every statement cancels out the next, and the timeline turns to static.

During the interviews, Ander tells Omar the lie about seeing someone else was just that, meant to push him away so Omar wouldn't have to watch him die. Omar doesn't forgive it easily. He tells Ander he got exactly what he wanted and walks out. Lu nearly breaks in her own interview, on the verge of confessing, until the Inspector needles her about not really being Polo's friend. That holds her together. She tells the Inspector, through tears, that whatever else was true, Polo was her friend, and so were all of them.

By morning, some things settle. Guzman and Nadia finally kiss. Samuel and Carla share a last kiss on the cheek before she leaves to study abroad. Omar leaves with Malick, Ander left standing alone. Rebeka goes home and tells her mother she's done with the drug business for good.

At the station, the case resolves itself when Polo's own parents hand over the real murder weapon, the trophy he used to kill Marina, and lie to the Inspector, claiming Polo confessed before he died rather than let the investigation reopen and risk the truth about Lu coming out. At his funeral, Cayetana turns down his parents' offer to pay for her education, telling them she's done pretending to be something she's not. Carla hands the family winery over to Valerio to run while she's away.

Time passes. Nadia and Omar are about to board a flight to New York when Omar realizes he can't leave Ander behind, and turns back for the hospital, arriving just in time to learn Ander's cancer is in remission. Omar's father, finally coming around, refers to Ander as his son's boyfriend without flinching. Lu, cut off by her own parents for good, is taken in by Nadia's family. Guzman leaves Nadia a voicemail promising to wait for her.

Two months later, most of them walk back into Las Encinas for one final year: Samuel, Guzman, Rebeka, Omar, Ander. Cayetana isn't among them as a student anymore. She's the school's new janitor, mopping the same halls she once faked her way through.
