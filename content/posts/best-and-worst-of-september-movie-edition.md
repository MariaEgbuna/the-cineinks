September was a big month for movies. I watched 10, dropped one, and found my movie of the year along the way. I also found a few that didn't deserve the hype. Here's how it went.

- - -

## September's Wins

![Best Movies](/images/posts/top-movies-1.jpg)

**1. Project Hail Mary (2026): 10.0**

Best movie of the year for me. Go watch it.

**2. Fantastic Mr. Fox (2009): 9.0**

It's the oldest movie in my top five, and it beat almost every new release I watched this month. 'Twas a fun ride from start to finish.

**3. The Invite (2026): 7.5**

Sometimes a movie is fine and you still don't connect with it. That's The Invite. It's just not for me.

**4. The Naked Gun (2025): 7.4**

It's a goofy movie and it doesn't try to be anything more.

**5. Free Fall (2013): 7.3**

Well-acted and quietly devastating. I never regretted watching it. [Read my full thoughts on Free Fall](https://cineinks.vercel.app/posts/my-thoughts-on-free-fall).

## The Overhyped One

![The Furious](/images/posts/furious.jpg)

**The Furious (2026): 7.2**

A decent flick, but it didn't live up to the noise around it. [Here's why I think it's overhyped](https://cineinks.vercel.app/posts/the-furious-is-overhyped).

## The Worst of September

![Worst movies I have watched](/images/posts/bottom-movies.jpg)

**1. The Runner (2026): 4.8**

I already dropped my take on this one. [My full review of The Runner](https://cineinks.vercel.app/posts/review-the-runner-2026).

**2. Green Lantern (2011): 5.4**

I decided to watch this after enjoying HBO's Lanterns and wanting more Green Lantern in my life. I'd never seen the Ryan Reynolds one, so I gave it a shot. It's just an average film overall, but the CGI was actually awful.

**3. The Kitchen (2019): 6.5**

A decent film, nothing exceptional. I watched it because Melissa McCarthy was in it.

## Movies I Gave Up On

![Unfrosted](/images/posts/unfrosted.jpg)

**Unfrosted (2024)**

Took me two days, just to watch half of it. It was so stupid, that my love for Melissa couldn't save it.

- - -

And that's the end of my list. Thank you for reading.
