*The Manipulated* was supposed to be a revenge story, but the ending completely failed to deliver the one thing I was waiting for: revenge.

The Disney+ K-drama is a reworking of Ji Chang-wook's 2017 film *Fabricated City*. Written by Oh Sang-ho (*Taxi Driver*) and directed by Park Shin-woo and Kim Chang-ju, it stars Ji and Do Kyung-soo, with Lee Kwang-soo, Kim Jong-soo and Jo Yoon-su.

*This post contains major spoilers for The Manipulated, including how it ends.*

---

## The Premise

![Ji Chang-wook as Park Tae-joong](/images/posts/review-the-manipulated/image-1.jpg "Ji Chang-wook as Park Tae-joong")

Park Tae-joong is a hardworking, kind man who makes deliveries while preparing to open his own plant-themed cafe. One day, he finds a lost phone and does the decent thing by returning it. Soon after, he's arrested for a horrific crime he didn't commit, and a mounting pile of fabricated evidence sends him to prison.

When Tae-joong discovers he was set up, he sets out to find who framed him and make them pay.

## The Good

The first four episodes are fast, intense and wild but still believable, leaving you eager to see Tae-joong get his revenge, and Ji Chang-wook's performance is another plus.

## The Bad

![Do Kyung-soo as An Yo-han](/images/posts/review-the-manipulated/image-2.jpg "Do Kyung-soo as An Yo-han")

* The villains bored the hell out of me because all they do is murder. Yo-han is the worst, a one-dimensional man who kills because he likes it, with no nuance, and Do Kyung-soo comes off forced in the role.

* It all went downhill once the prisoners were let loose for a game. And by episodes 7 and 8, it was nonstop car chases, plot armor, lucky breaks for the lead and choppy fight scenes.

* The show also skips past what could have given the story some depth. A man is wrongfully convicted, but there's no real focus on the mistaken evidence AT ALL.

* The ending felt anticlimactic, and there were so many plot holes. Yong-sik, Tae-joong's friend from prison, gets framed for the assemblyman's death, and no one checks for his alibi? Also, what happened to Yo-han's mom? The show reveals that his nanny is his biological mother, then she vanishes before the story wraps up.

* Yo-han destroyed Tae-joong's life, yet there's no satisfying punishment or reckoning. He isn't brought to his knees and he never faces the same kind of suffering he put Tae-joong through. He dies in the end, but for a revenge story, that's simply not enough. I needed to see him lose his power, realize that everything was over and face the consequences, preferably behind bars.

---

With fewer episodes, more plot and a villain with more than one side to him, this could have been a taut revenge story. Instead, it threw away a great start and left me waiting for a payback that never felt complete. Such a shame.
