Most alien invasion movies give you a faceless threat that drops out of the sky and starts killing people. *Hope* doesn't. Na Hong-jin's 2026 sci-fi film sets itself up that way, then flips it: the aliens aren't invading, they're looking for a missing child.

*This post contains full spoilers for Hope (2026), including the ending and the mid-credits scene.*

## How does the conflict start?

![Alien Appearance](/images/posts/hope-ending-explained/first-alien.jpg)

Police chief Bum-seok (Hwang Jung-min) is called to a scene with a mutilated bull. The local hunters, led by Sung-ki (Zo In-sung), assume a tiger is loose in the mountains. Then they find a tall, sharp-toothed humanoid hunting them instead.

Midway through, the film shows what set this off. Before the movie begins, a villager named Yang-bae shot a small creature in the woods and hid the body in his freezer. The tall alien belongs to an imperial civilization, and it is on an emergency rescue mission to recover the empress's missing child.

The town makes it worse. Hope Harbor sits right along the DMZ, and after decades of cold conflict the locals expect invasion, so they arm up with illegal, military-grade weapons and attack. Information only moves through broken radio signals and rumors, so neither side sees the full picture. Every defensive move by the humans looks like an attack to the aliens.

## What does the ending mean?

![Aliens in Hope](/images/posts/hope-ending-explained/aliens.jpg)

In the third act, a massive alien spaceship crashes into the terrain, and for the first time in the 160-minute runtime the alien dialogue gets subtitles. We meet Empress J'aur (Alicia Vikander) and her bodyguard Ma'veyyo (Michael Fassbender).

With their child running out of time, Ma'veyyo rips out his own heart to use as an extra organ to revive him. The film ends with the empress looking over the destruction and saying **"Change fate."** Na Hong-jin has confirmed he left the mythology open on purpose to set up sequels.

## What happens in the credits?

Sung-ki is seemingly killed earlier in a vehicle crash, but the mid-credits scene shows he survived, limping down an empty road. After all the tragedy, a bit of *hope* is still walking around.
