This list came together differently than my last one. I stumbled backward into these picks: a few found me through random Twitter clips, one had so much hype that I went in ready to hate-watch it (and failed), and a couple pushed me out of my usual genres.

## 1. Interviews with Monster Girls (2017)

![Anime Recommendations: 5 Shows I Wasn't Expecting to Enjoy This Much](/images/posts/5-anime-i-wasnt-expecting-to-enjoy-this-much/interview-with-monster-girls.jpg)

**2017 • 1 Season • 12 Episodes (+ 1 OVA) • Completed**

Tetsuo Takahashi is a high school biology teacher fascinated by "demis," demi-humans like vampires, dullahans, and succubi who just want ordinary lives. When a few demi students enroll at his school, he finally gets to interview them.

I saw a funny clip on my timeline, expected a forgettable comedy, and ended up charmed. The show works because of how respectfully Takahashi treats his students. Under the supernatural gags, it is a warm look at growing up "different" in a world that wasn't built for you, and it never slips into uncomfortable anime tropes.

**What could have been better:** Honestly? No complaints. It's a tight, short watch.

## 2. Orb: On the Movement of the Earth (2024–2025)

![Anime Recommendations: 5 Shows I Wasn't Expecting to Enjoy This Much](/images/posts/5-anime-i-wasnt-expecting-to-enjoy-this-much/orb-on-the-movement-of-earth.jpg)

**2024–2025 • 1 Season • 25 Episodes • Completed**

In a brutal medieval Europe, a chain of thinkers risk torture and execution by the Church to prove heliocentrism, the idea that the Earth moves around the Sun.

The internet was screaming about this show while it aired, and the hype still wasn't enough. The tension never lets up, and watching ordinary people sacrifice everything for a truth they will never see proven moved me. It changed how I think about human progress and the unnamed people who died so we could understand our place in the universe. If you watch only one show on this list, make it this one.

**What could have been better:** The story wraps up beautifully in episode 24, which leaves episode 25 feeling like a tonal curveball.

## 3. My Happy Marriage (2023–2025)

![Anime Recommendations: 5 Shows I Wasn't Expecting to Enjoy This Much](/images/posts/5-anime-i-wasnt-expecting-to-enjoy-this-much/my-happy-marriage.jpg)

**2023–2025 (Special coming Oct 2026) • 2 Seasons • 26 Episodes (+ 3-episode special) • Ongoing**

A dark, supernatural take on Cinderella. Miyo Saimori, raised and abused by her stepfamily, is married off to Kiyoka Kudo, a cold, feared military commander, and expects a nightmare.

I am not a romance person, but I promised myself I would step out of my comfort zone for this list. It isn't my favorite here, but once the supernatural lore settles in and Miyo starts healing, it becomes compelling and looks stunning. If you like historical romance, you will probably lose your mind over this one.

**What could have been better:** Season two takes a hit. Once the central couple settles, the emotional stakes feel diluted and the pacing slows.

## 4. Kaguya-sama: Love Is War (2019–2022)

![Anime Recommendations: 5 Shows I Wasn't Expecting to Enjoy This Much](/images/posts/5-anime-i-wasnt-expecting-to-enjoy-this-much/kaguya-sama-love-is-war.jpg)

**2019–2022 • 3 Seasons • 37 Episodes (+ film) • On Hiatus**

Student council elites Kaguya Shinomiya and Miyuki Shirogane are in love, but both are too proud to confess first. So they treat romance like a military campaign and wage psychological warfare to make the other one crack.

I don't do romance, but this is the exception, because it is secretly a comedy disguised as a love story. Every scheme spirals out of control in the most ridiculous way, the supporting cast is legendary, and it never gets repetitive. Ten out of ten, no notes.

**What could have been better:** Nothing about the show. My only grievance is the radio silence from A-1 Pictures. I need a season four announcement.

## 5. The Apothecary Diaries (2023–Present)

![Anime Recommendations: 5 Shows I Wasn't Expecting to Enjoy This Much](/images/posts/5-anime-i-wasnt-expecting-to-enjoy-this-much/the-apothecary-diaries.jpg)

**2023–Present (Season 3 starting Oct 2026) • 2 Seasons • 48 Episodes • Ongoing**

Maomao, a sharp, poison-obsessed apothecary's daughter, is kidnapped and sold into the Imperial Palace. After she secretly solves a mysterious illness plaguing the Emperor's children, she catches the eye of Jinshi, a powerful palace official who pulls her into court intrigue.

I lost a weekend to this show and binged the whole first season in under three days. It is the most gorgeous production on this list, but the real draw is Maomao: pragmatic, cynical, and happy to test deadly toxins on her own arm. The mystery-of-the-week format keeps the pacing crisp.

**What could have been better:** My only regret is waiting so long to press play because I assumed it was another slow, forgettable palace drama.

---

Monster comedy with real emotional depth, a romance that got past my cynical side, a historical thriller that left me reeling. Every one of these blindsided me. If you're tired of the same three seasonal hits, add these to your watchlist.
