This list came together a little differently than my last one. 

Instead of intentionally tracking down a masterpiece, I practically stumbled backward into these picks. A few of them found me via random Twitter clips, one had so much overwhelming hype that I went in fully prepared to hate-watch it (and failed), and a couple forced me to completely step outside my usual genre lanes. 

If you're looking for something that hits entirely different than whatever is currently dominating your feed, these are absolutely worth your time.

## 1. Interviews with Monster Girls (2017)

![Anime Recommendations: 5 Shows I Wasn't Expecting to Enjoy This Much](/images/posts/5-anime-i-wasnt-expecting-to-enjoy-this-much/interview-with-monster-girls.jpg)

**2017 • 1 Season • 12 Episodes (+ 1 OVA) • Completed**

*Interviews with Monster Girls* follows Tetsuo Takahashi, a high school biology teacher fascinated by "demis" — demi-humans like vampires, dullahans, and succubi trying to live quiet, ordinary lives. When a handful of demi students enroll at his school, he finally gets the chance to interview them and learn how their biology works.

I saw a funny clip on my timeline, expected a quick, forgettable, throwaway comedy, and ended up completely charmed. What makes this show work so well is the genuine, respectful dynamic Takahashi has with his students. Beneath the supernatural gags, it's actually a beautifully empathetic look at what it feels like to grow up "different" in a world that wasn't built to accommodate you. It is warm, funny, and incredibly grounded without ever tipping into uncomfortable anime tropes.

*   **What could have been better:** Honestly? No complaints. It’s a tight, short watch that doesn't waste a single episode.

## 2. Orb: On the Movement of the Earth (2024–2025)

![Anime Recommendations: 5 Shows I Wasn't Expecting to Enjoy This Much](/images/posts/5-anime-i-wasnt-expecting-to-enjoy-this-much/orb-on-the-movement-of-earth.jpg)

**2024–2025 • 1 Season • 25 Episodes • Completed**

Set in a fictional, brutal medieval Europe, this generational epic follows a chain of brilliant thinkers who risk torture and execution by the Church to prove heliocentrism — the heretical idea that the Earth moves around the Sun. 

Where do I even start with this one? The internet was screaming about this show while it was airing, and if you ask me, the hype still wasn't enough. It is an absolute masterclass from start to finish. The tension is relentless, and watching ordinary human beings sacrifice everything for a scientific truth they will never live to see vindicated is profoundly moving. It completely changed how I think about human progress and the invisible, unnamed people who died just so we could understand our place in the universe. If you watch only one show on this list, make it this one.

*   **What could have been better:** The finale structure is a bit jarring. The core narrative wraps up beautifully in episode 24, which leaves episode 25 feeling like a bit of a tonal curveball that slightly disrupts the perfect closure of the previous episode.

## 3. My Happy Marriage (2023–2025)

![Anime Recommendations: 5 Shows I Wasn't Expecting to Enjoy This Much](/images/posts/5-anime-i-wasnt-expecting-to-enjoy-this-much/my-happy-marriage.jpg)

**2023–2025 (Special coming Oct 2026) • 2 Seasons • 26 Episodes (+ 3-episode special) • Ongoing**

A dark, supernatural twist on the classic Cinderella story. Miyo Saimori is an unpowered young woman raised and abused by her stepfamily. When she is married off to Kiyoka Kudo —a notoriously cold, feared military commander— she expects a nightmare, only for the arrangement to slowly transform her life in ways she never saw coming.

Look, I am notoriously *not* a romance person, but I promised myself I would step out of my comfort zone for this list. While it isn't my absolute favorite of the bunch, I have to give it credit: once the supernatural lore settles in and Miyo starts healing from her trauma, the story becomes incredibly compelling and visually stunning. If you actually enjoy historical romance, you will probably lose your mind over this.

*   **What could have been better:** Season two takes a bit of a hit. Once the central couple's relationship stabilizes, the emotional stakes feel a bit more diluted, and the plot pacing slows down compared to the breakout first season.

## 4. Kaguya-sama: Love Is War (2019–2022)

![Anime Recommendations: 5 Shows I Wasn't Expecting to Enjoy This Much](/images/posts/5-anime-i-wasnt-expecting-to-enjoy-this-much/kaguya-sama-love-is-war.jpg)

**2019–2022 • 3 Seasons • 37 Episodes (+ film) • On Hiatus**

Student council elites Kaguya Shinomiya and Miyuki Shirogane are hopelessly, desperately in love with each other. The catch? They are both far too arrogant to admit it first. Instead, they treat romance like a high-stakes military campaign, waging psychological warfare to trick the other into confessing first.

Again, I don't do romance, but *Kaguya-sama* is the magnificent exception to the rule because it is secretly a high-octane comedy disguised as a love story. The sheer psychological absurdity of their mind games is endlessly entertaining. Every single scheme spirals out of control in the most ridiculous way possible, the supporting cast is legendary, and the show somehow never gets repetitive. Ten out of ten, absolutely no notes. 

*   **What could have been better:** The show itself is flawless. My only real grievance is the current radio silence from A-1 Pictures — we desperately need a season four announcement.

## 5. The Apothecary Diaries (2023–Present)

![Anime Recommendations: 5 Shows I Wasn't Expecting to Enjoy This Much](/images/posts/5-anime-i-wasnt-expecting-to-enjoy-this-much/the-apothecary-diaries.jpg)

**2023–Present (Season 3 starting Oct 2026) • 2 Seasons • 48 Episodes • Ongoing**

When Maomao, a sharp, poison-obsessed apothecary's daughter, is kidnapped and sold into servitude within the Imperial Palace, she tries to keep her head down. But after she uses her medical knowledge to secretly solve a bizarre illness plaguing the Emperor's children, she catches the eye of Jinshi — a beautiful, influential palace official who drags her into the dangerous world of court intrigue.

I completely lost a weekend to this show and binged the entire first season in less than three days. Visually, it is easily the most gorgeous, vibrant production on this entire list. But the real selling point is Maomao herself. Her deeply pragmatic, cynical outlook and obsession with testing deadly toxins on her own arm make her one of the most refreshing and entertaining protagonists in modern anime. The medical mystery-of-the-week format keeps the pacing incredibly crisp.

*   **What could have been better:** Honestly, my only regret is waiting so long to press play because I foolishly assumed it would just be another slow, forgettable palace drama. 

---

Whether it was a monster comedy with unexpected emotional depth, a romance that managed to break through my cynical exterior, or a historical thriller that left me reeling, every single one of these shows completely blindsided me. 

If you're tired of watching the same three seasonal hits everyone else is talking about, do yourself a favor and add these to your watchlist.
