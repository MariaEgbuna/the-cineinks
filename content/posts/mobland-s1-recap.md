*MobLand* opens with Harry Da Souza, fixer for London's Harrigan crime family, trying to keep the peace while patriarch Conrad Harrigan eyes a rival's fentanyl trade. Across 10 episodes, one missing young man turns that tension into a war, with traitors and police closing in.

*This post contains spoilers for Season 1 of Paramount+'s MobLand.*

*Content note: This recap mentions sexual abuse, including abuse in prison and within a family, and graphic violence.*

### The Quick Version (TL;DR)

* **The Setup:** Conrad's grandson Eddie goes out with Tommy Stevenson, son of rival boss Richie Stevenson, and Tommy never comes home. Harry has to clean up the mess as Conrad pushes into the fentanyl business.
* **The Mystery:** Someone is leaking the family's secrets. Conrad kills his old friend Archie as a suspected traitor, but the leaks continue.
* **The Twist:** Maeve, Conrad's wife, is behind two major betrayals, while family lawyer O'Hara has been feeding Richie information. Harry and Kevin, Conrad's son, wipe out Richie and O'Hara, but Conrad and Maeve end the season in custody, and Jan, Harry's wife, accidentally stabs him.

---

## Eddie's Night Out and Tommy's Disappearance

![Harry Da Souza](/images/posts/mobland-s1-recap/image-1.jpg "Tom Hardy as Harry Da Souza in “MobLand.” (Paramount+)")

When a truce between the Dogan and Lazaros families looks doomed, Harry follows Conrad's orders and wipes out both sides. Meanwhile, Eddie stabs a man named Hughie during a night out with Tommy. Harry gets club owner Valjon to delete the CCTV footage and scares Hughie into silence, but Tommy is now missing. Richie wants answers.

Conrad also wants to take over Richie's fentanyl business. His longtime adviser Archie objects, and after Maeve accuses him of betrayal, Conrad shoots him dead. Police soon question Harry about Archie's disappearance.

Harry eventually pressures Valjon, who leads him to Tommy's dismembered body. Eddie killed Tommy and paid Valjon to get rid of the evidence. Eddie's parents, Kevin and Bella, are pulled deeper into the crisis after a bomb hits their house.

To protect Eddie, Harry and Kevin force Valjon to take the blame by using his children as leverage. Harry later kills Valjon in front of Richie to sell the story. Richie appears to accept it, then asks the Harrigans to attend Tommy's funeral.

The family has other problems:

* **Old wounds:** Harry and Kevin met in prison. Kevin is still haunted by the sexual abuse he suffered there from prison officer Alan Rusby, who resurfaces in the present.
* **Family secrets:** Bella had an affair with Conrad, and she is pursuing risky business of her own with her associate Antoine, involving her father's government connections.
* **A new friend:** Jan grows close to Alice, a woman from therapy who is secretly working for the police. Conrad also visits Jan and warns her to stay quiet about the family.

## The Funeral and the Antwerp Betrayal

![The Harrigans](/images/posts/mobland-s1-recap/image-2.jpg "The Harrigans: Helen Mirren, Pierce Brosnan and Anson Boon (Paramount+)")

The Harrigans go to the funeral with weapons smuggled in, just in case. Police are watching too, thanks to details Jan didn't realise she was giving away. Maeve and Eddie provoke the Stevensons, and Harry and Kevin drug Maeve to stop things getting worse.

In private, Richie admits he never believed Valjon did it. He doesn't want a war, but if the Stevensons can get to Eddie, he's fair game. Maeve wrecks that uneasy deal by having Richie's wife, Vron, killed with a car bomb. Conrad covers for her and declares war.

Conrad's daughter Seraphina and his eldest son Brendan are in Antwerp for a diamond deal. Maeve, Seraphina's stepmother, secretly gives Richie their location, offering up Seraphina on the condition that Brendan is spared. The deal turns into a massacre, and both siblings are taken.

Harry fights his way across Antwerp as Richie streams their planned execution to the family. With time running out, Harry asks Kat McAllister, a powerful crime figure who has been trying to reach him, for help. Cartel boss Jaime Lopez's men kill Brendan before Kat's call stops them. Seraphina survives, and Harry now owes Kat.

## Rats, Police and a Family Dinner

![The tense Harrigan family dinner with Alice in MobLand Season 1](/images/posts/mobland-s1-recap/image-3.jpg)

Conrad wants a fentanyl deal with Jaime, so Harry asks Kat to broker a meeting, putting himself further in her debt. Harry also presses Freddie, his source inside Richie's operation, and learns that someone close to the Harrigans has been working with Richie. Then he throws Freddie to his death.

Detectives Fisk and Mukasa, who have been after the family, offer Richie immunity in return for help bringing down Conrad. Richie kills them both. Former senior officer Colin Tattersall, who suggested the deal, turns out to be working for Richie.

Tattersall then pushes Alice (real name Nicola) to collect what he needs to frame Conrad and Maeve. Jaime agrees to the deal, but only if Harry deals with the family's rat.

Kevin, meanwhile, tracks down Rusby and kills him.

Conrad invites Alice to dinner, where the family falls apart. After a confession from Eddie, Conrad exposes Maeve for revealing where Archie's body was and leading Richie to Seraphina and Brendan. Harry catches Alice taking Maeve's glass, with Conrad's cigar inside, but lets her leave with it. Armed police then arrest Conrad and Maeve on suspicion of murder.

## Kevin Takes Charge and the War Ends

![Harry and Kevin](/images/posts/mobland-s1-recap/image-4.jpg "Tom Hardy and Paddy Considine (Paramount+)")

Harry cleans up after Rusby's killing, then confronts O'Hara, who admits she's been feeding Richie information, though she says she never told him about Archie or Antwerp. Kevin visits Conrad in prison, tells him Archie was never the rat and makes it clear that things are changing.

Maeve tells Eddie that Conrad, not Kevin, is his father. She urges him to kill Harry and Seraphina so he can take control. Eddie confronts Bella and tries to choke her, but Kiko, one of Harry's closest associates, pulls him off. Elsewhere, Tattersall betrays Alice, who is drugged and driven away while he tells Richie the job is done.

Harry uses O'Hara to lure Richie into a trap. Richie splits his men between her office and the Harrigans' safehouse. Paul, a Harrigan henchman, blows up the office with Richie's men inside. At the safehouse, Kiko and Harry's other close associate, Zosia, help defeat the second group, but Kiko is shot dead.

Harry and Kevin go after Richie. Kevin kills him, and Harry finishes off O'Hara. They also have Alice on tape admitting she tampered with evidence, which could help free Conrad and Maeve.

Kat then asks Harry to help her take down the Harrigans. He refuses, even when she makes it clear that this will make them enemies. Back home, Jan's anger over what Harry's work has done to their family boils over. While arguing as she chops vegetables, she cuts herself, lashes out as Harry tries to help and accidentally stabs him in the chest.

## Where We Left Everyone

* **Harry & Jan:** He turns down Kat and makes an enemy of her, then goes home, where Jan's anger boils over and he ends the season with her accidental knife in his chest.
* **Kevin & Bella:** After sharing their histories of abuse, they agree to stand together as Kevin prepares to lead.
* **Eddie:** He now knows Conrad is his father, and Maeve wants him to seize control.
* **Conrad & Maeve:** Both are still in custody. Conrad enjoys a hero's reception from other inmates, while the pair look for a way out.
* **Seraphina:** She is open to meeting Kat about the future.
* **Zosia:** She survives the attack on the safehouse.
* **Kiko:** He dies in the safehouse fight.
* **Richie & O'Hara:** Both are killed by Kevin and Harry's ambush.
* **Alice:** Tattersall betrays her, and she is last seen being drugged and taken away.
