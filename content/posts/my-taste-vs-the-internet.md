Loving a movie the internet hates is a weird feeling. So is sitting through one everyone calls the greatest thing since sliced bread and feeling nothing. Then there are the box office bombs, the childhood favorites that fell apart on rewatch, and the ones I gave a chance and regretted.

I watched or rewatched a bunch of movies recently, and I have thoughts on all of them. This was kinda fun to write.

---

## The One Everyone Hates And I Don't

### Shark Tale (2004)

![Shark Tale](/images/posts/my-taste-vs-the-internet/shark-tale.jpg)

Shark Tale is a movie I loved as a kid. I rewatched it recently and it was still so damn funny. Then I went online and found out it's hated. Someone even made a YouTube video – a whole video – calling it a cinematic disaster.

Come on! That's so harsh. I don't get the hate, and I still don't, till today.

---

## The Rewatches That Didn't Survive

### Shrek the Third (2007)

![Shrek the Third](/images/posts/my-taste-vs-the-internet/shrek-the-third.jpg)

Shrek the Third is a tricky one because I watched and enjoyed it as a kid. A few weeks ago I tried rewatching it and couldn't make it past the 40 minute mark. Maybe it's because I'd just come off the high of the second movie – probably one of the best sequels ever. Whatever the reason, I don't like Shrek 3 anymore.

### Krrish 3 (2013)

![Krrish 3](/images/posts/my-taste-vs-the-internet/krrish-3.jpg)

This one is a different kind of sad. I remember thinking it was a decent flick when I watched it over a decade ago, so I rewatched it a few months ago and boyyyyy. It sucks. It really does. And that hurts me, because I love Hrithik Roshan. This movie failed on almost every level: the villain was terrible, some of the acting looked goofy, and the plot was all over the place. For my mental health, this movie doesn't exist anymore in 3, 2, 1.....

---

## The Old Favorite That Still Holds Up

### Dragon Tiger Gate (2006)

![Dragon Tiger Gate](/images/posts/my-taste-vs-the-internet/dragon-tiger-gate.jpg)

This movie is almost 20 years old, and it stars Donnie Yen, Nicholas Tse and Shawn Yue. I watched it so many times as a kid – with no subs – and it was really good. I watched it again recently, with some questionable subs, and lemme tell you right now, I still love it. It's still as cool as I remembered. The action scenes made me giddy from start to finish.

I can admit the hairstyles were ridiculous, the third act gets a little goofy toward the end, and the CG and effects didn't age well, but I don't care. This movie is fucking awesome.

---

## The Hyped Ones I Don't Get

### Corpse Bride (2005)

![Corpse Bride](/images/posts/my-taste-vs-the-internet/corpse-bride.jpg)

Corpse Bride has really high ratings everywhere online, but I found it quite average – boring, even. It's a decent film, but I don't care for it. I slept off twice, and it took me roughly 3 days to finish.

### The Ministry of Ungentlemanly Warfare (2024)

![The Ministry of Ungentlemanly Warfare](/images/posts/my-taste-vs-the-internet/the-ministry-of-ungentlemanly-warfare.jpg)

It's so average. Aura and good looks alone can't save a movie. That's all I'll say.

### I Saw the TV Glow (2024)

![I Saw the TV Glow](/images/posts/my-taste-vs-the-internet/i-saw-the-tv-glow.jpg)

I think I hate this movie. I found it dull, pretentious, and very lackluster. Trash.
