Premiering on Netflix in April 2022, the eight-episode fifth season of Elite continues the mystery surrounding Armando's disappearance while introducing new students Isadora and Iván to Las Encinas. Told through flash-forwards to Samuel's police confession and a poolside medical emergency, the season follows the unraveling of Benjamín's hidden dealings.

_This post contains spoilers for Season 5 of Netflix's Elite._

_Content note: this recap mentions sexual assault, drugging, and an adult pursuing a relationship with a teenager._

---

### Elodie's video, Samuel's confession

The season opens mid-apology. Phillipe's trying to make things right with Cayetana after last season when the lights cut out and a video starts playing. It's Elodie, the girl he confessed to abusing in a voice message back in Season 4, and this time she's not keeping it quiet. She tells everyone watching that Phillipe raped her.

Then the show jumps to the police station, where Samuel sits across from the Inspector and confesses to murder, with a body already shown floating face-down in a swimming pool before the show explains whose it is.

It rewinds to the start of the school year, just after Elodie's video goes public. Las Encinas responds by locking phones away and enforcing strict distance between students, though it does little to contain the fallout. Phillipe becomes public enemy number one overnight. Someone slips a pair of scissors into his bag with a note telling him to castrate himself. He's technically been cleared of charges before, and Benjamín addresses the school directly, calling the treatment against him unacceptable. Phillipe asks Cayetana to publicly vouch for him. The one person on his side without being asked is a new student, Isadora.

Patrick's dealing with his own mess at home: a blowup with his father sends him to Omar for comfort, and the two sleep together that same afternoon while Ander, off on the other side of the world, stays silent and unreachable. Patrick also starts circling a new guy, Ivan, who insists he's not gay while stripping down in front of him in the locker room. Ivan's father, Cruz, is constantly out partying instead of around. One afternoon at Ivan's place, things get physical between the two boys, and when Patrick slips off to the bathroom alone, Cruz catches him there and, instead of looking away, gives him an encouraging thumbs up.

At school, Mencía tells the Inspector she was assaulted, on top of everything already unraveling around Ari. Benjamín blames himself for not watching Armando more closely, and Samuel assures him Armando isn't coming back. Cayetana, nudged along by Isadora, goes on camera and publicly stands by Phillipe, saying she doesn't want him thrown out of school.

The episode closes back in the interview room, where Samuel admits he killed someone.

---

### Benjamín tightens his grip

Back in the interview room, Rebe tells the Inspector that Samuel didn't actually kill anyone. Meanwhile Benjamín's grip on the school tightens: strict rules, phones locked away, and it comes out that he's tracking students' locations through the school app. When they push back, he threatens to tank their grades. Patrick leads the pushback, throwing a party partly to get a moment with Ivan, while Cayetana and Phillipe stay out of it. Cayetana also pushes Phillipe to see the school counselor, Greta, who bans him from drinking and calls him a predator.

Benjamín walks in on Ari and Samuel having sex and drags both into his office separately, Samuel's scholarship on the line again. Meanwhile Omar takes in a homeless teenager named Bilal, offering him a dishwashing job out of pity, only for Bilal to try stealing the tip jar the same day. Omar throws him out, unmoved by his claims that dangerous people are after him.

At Patrick's party, things spiral. Rebe and Ivan kiss on the dance floor, caught on video and sent straight to Mencía. Patrick, crushed watching it unfold, gets Ari to try drugs, and when he confronts Ivan about hiding who he really is, Ivan responds by kissing Samuel in front of him.

Benjamín escalates by handing out drug tests tied to grades, with positive results meaning expulsion. The students revolt and toss the tests, so Benjamín turns them against each other with an anonymous vote to expel one of their own. It comes down to Samuel and Omar, and Omar votes for Samuel out loud, getting him kicked out for three days and shattering whatever trust was left between them. Samuel throws him out of the house that night.

### A body in the lake

Bilal keeps circling back, and during a lakeside fundraiser Samuel catches him pickpocketing a guest and kicks him out again, right as Omar, following Samuel outside, spots a body floating in the lake and dives in convinced it's Bilal. It isn't. It's Armando, killed and dumped at the end of last season, finally surfacing. Bilal shows up to help pull the body out. Rebe, Omar, and Bilal dispose of it together on the beach, with Omar furious nobody told him the truth sooner. Eventually Samuel calls the police himself, and the season's opening flash-forward gets its twist: the body in the pool everyone's been building toward is Samuel.

Phillipe drifts further from Cayetana and toward Isadora, eventually breaking things off with Cayetana and kissing Isadora in full view of everyone. Patrick catches Ari and Ivan having sex out on the water and falls apart, and Cruz comforts him. The two end up kissing. Rebe and Mencía's relationship keeps fracturing, worsened when Mencía sees Rebe with another girl, Jess, at a drive-in. In the chaos of the fundraiser, Mencía finds a second SIM card hidden in her own phone, planted there by Armando, and traces the number on it back to her father.

Ivan and Patrick, bonding over shared father issues, finally sleep together. Cruz tries to pull Patrick into his room but gets stood up when Patrick chooses Ivan. Benjamín gets pressed by the Inspector over the investigation Mencía's SIM card ties into, and has Mencía hide a second phone for him, unaware the Inspector's watching on camera. Isadora's drinking spirals hard enough that Cayetana takes her home, and later, with Isadora finally sober enough to talk, gets a glimpse of how lonely her life is underneath the chaos.

Cruz confronts Patrick in front of Benjamín: "I waited for you." Before it can go further, police arrive to search Benjamín's office. In the scramble, Mencía's caught stashing his second phone, and just before she can say anything to the investigator, Samuel bursts in and confesses to Armando's murder himself. It comes out that Benjamín had already offered him a deal beforehand: take the fall, and Benjamín would make sure his life afterward was taken care of.

### Samuel takes the fall

Samuel plays along at first, coached by Benjamín to stay calm while bail gets arranged. The group scrambles to raise the twenty-five thousand euros needed to get him out, and it's revealed the SIM card was never Benjamín's: Armando hid it in Mencía's phone because it was the last place anyone would check. Mencía's already made her own copy of everything incriminating, not fully trusting her father either. Ivan, moved by how much Ari clearly cares about Samuel, decides to help raise the bail money, especially once Cruz, learning Ivan slept with Patrick, refuses to contribute out of spite.

The group throws a charity auction, "Buy Your Valentine," which pairs Ivan with Ari and Patrick with Cruz, the two couples splitting off privately. Ivan and Ari end up kissing. Patrick and Cruz end up in a bathtub together, and later a pool, which Ivan catches sight of before sending Ari away. Samuel finally walks free, thanking Benjamín warmly for coming through, until Ari tells him it wasn't Benjamín's money that got him out. It was Ivan's. The episode flashes forward to Patrick standing over Samuel, covered in blood.

By episode seven, the investigator is pushing Samuel harder, laying out evidence that Benjamín and Armando had been trafficking blackmail material on powerful people. He offers Samuel a way out: help build the case against Benjamín in exchange for his own freedom. Ivan, furious over what happened with Patrick, confronts both his father and Patrick directly, calling Patrick manipulative and demanding he stay out of his life. A flash cuts back to that bloody night: Samuel crawling away while an injured Patrick calls out for help.

Back at school, Rebe learns about the SIM card and convinces Cayetana and Mencía to help track it down as the evidence that could clear Samuel and take Benjamín down. Cayetana breaks into the school at night to search Benjamín's office while Rebe searches his home, and it's ultimately Mencía who hands it over, telling Rebe she loves her and refuses to let an innocent man go to prison over her father's secrets. Benjamín gets tipped off that Cayetana was caught snooping around his office the same night.

Phillipe tells Isadora that no amount of glitter and money fills the emptiness underneath, refusing to date her while she's still spiraling. She responds by flying out to a party in Ibiza via helicopter, and Phillipe follows. During a dinner at Benjamín's house, Patrick shoves him in a moment of rage over everything with Ivan and Cruz, and later admits to Cruz how conflicted falling for him has left him. They kiss again. A drunk Ivan leaves a voicemail confessing his real feelings for Patrick, and by mistake sends it to Ari's phone.

In Ibiza, Isadora collapses at the party, unconscious. Phillipe, barely conscious himself, is slumped against a wall nearby when three men notice she's out cold and decide to take advantage of the situation, locking the door behind them.

### Ibiza, then the pool

Ari shows up late one night to see Rebe and Samuel and pulls Samuel aside. With the SIM card now in his hands, enough to bury her father, she tells him it's his call what to do with it, kisses him on the cheek, and leaves him to decide. Rebe reminds him afterward that Benjamín's never really valued him, just tolerated him, right up until he was ready to shake him off.

At school, Ivan and Phillipe both claim to remember only pieces of what happened in Ibiza, though Isadora's visibly traumatized, and Phillipe, given his own history, is terrified he might have been part of it. Ivan tells Patrick he's moving to Qatar with his father, and Patrick retreats into the showers alone. Isadora ends up there too, and when she starts bleeding, it becomes clear something far worse happened that night.

Cayetana, no longer employed at the school after getting caught snooping, finds her there and talks to her through the shower wall. Isadora breaks down the second she's alone.

The truth comes out through a video. One of the boys from that night filmed the whole thing and shows it off to Phillipe and Ivan. In it, Ivan tries to stop the three of them and collapses before he can. Phillipe crawls toward Isadora and passes out too. The boys assault her while both are down, and when Phillipe manages to lash out afterward, one of them mocks him over his own assault allegations, which is enough to make Phillipe hit him.

That night, Phillipe tells Isadora the truth, that he actually cares about her, and the two kiss as thunder rolls overhead. Around the same time, Cruz sits Patrick and Ivan down together and admits he can't keep doing this to either of them. He decides against Qatar, telling Ivan to go be happy and chase what he actually wants with Patrick.

Samuel heads to the police station with the SIM card, but Benjamín calls first, begging to talk in person. Samuel agrees. That night, Benjamín tries to buy him off: an Oxford business scholarship, tuition, housing, three thousand euros a month, in exchange for the SIM card and his silence about Armando. Samuel turns it down and tries to leave. Benjamín grabs for the bag, and in the struggle Samuel goes down hard, cracking his head against the edge of the pool and slipping into the water, blood spreading around him while Benjamín just watches, frozen.

Patrick finds them, pulls Samuel out, and calls everyone to tell them what's happened. Benjamín tries to stop them from calling the police.

Samuel survives. Barely conscious, he tells everyone the truth. The SIM card ends up lost in the pool water, and once Rebe realizes what happened, she goes after Benjamín herself, knocking him down before Mencía pulls her off.

The police finally arrive and arrest Benjamín, leading him away in handcuffs while Patrick watches, Ivan standing beside him. Patrick and Ari break down sobbing, while Omar and Rebe quietly walk away.

---

### Where we left everyone

* **Samuel:** Falls into the pool during a struggle with Benjamín, hits his head, and nearly drowns, but survives and names Benjamín as the one responsible.
* **Benjamín:** Arrested, handcuffed in front of his three children by the pool.
* **Patrick:** Pulls Samuel out of the pool and watches his father get taken away.
* **Ivan:** Stays in Spain instead of going to Qatar after Cruz tells him to follow his heart.
* **Cruz:** Steps aside and tells Ivan to chase what he wants with Patrick.
* **Rebe and Mencía:** Mencía hands Rebe the SIM card, then pulls her off Benjamín at the pool.
* **Ari:** Gives Samuel the SIM card and the choice of what to do with it.
* **Omar:** Walks away from the finale with Rebe.
* **Cayetana:** Loses her job at the school after being caught searching Benjamín's office.
* **Isadora:** Assaulted in Ibiza, and ends the season with Phillipe, who tells her he cares about her.
