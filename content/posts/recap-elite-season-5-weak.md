Premiering on Netflix in April 2022, the eight-episode fifth season of *Elite* continues the mystery surrounding Armando's disappearance while introducing new students Isadora and Iván to Las Encinas. Told through flash-forwards to Samuel's police confession and a poolside medical emergency, the season follows the unraveling of Benjamín's hidden dealings.

*This post contains spoilers for Season 5 of Netflix's Elite.*

*Content note: This recap mentions sexual assault, drugging, and an adult pursuing a relationship with a teenager.*

### The Quick Version (TL;DR)
* **The Fallout:** Elodie's leaked confession video turns Phillipe into an outcast, while Benjamín's strict new rules and location-tracking app spark a student revolt.
* **The Entanglements:** Patrick gets caught in a messy love triangle between new student Iván and Iván's famous soccer-player father, Cruz, while Armando's body surfaces in the lake.
* **The Finale:** Samuel takes the fall for Armando's murder before discovering a SIM card proving Benjamín's corruption, leading to a fatal struggle by Benjamín's pool.

---

## Elodie's Video and New Arrivals

The season opens with a public humiliation: Elodie, the girl Phillipe assaulted in Season 4, broadcasts her accusation to the entire school, turning Phillipe into a pariah overnight. Flash-forwards simultaneously show Samuel confessing to murder at the police station and a body floating face-down in a swimming pool.

Rewinding to the start of the semester, Benjamín locks students' phones away and tracks their locations through a school app, prompting Patrick to organize a rebellious party. Meanwhile, two wealthy newcomers shake up the student body:
* **Isadora:** A party-obsessed DJ heiress who immediately defends Phillipe and pushes Cayetana to publicly stand by him, though Isadora hides deep loneliness and substance abuse behind her glamorous image.
* **Iván:** The charming son of superstar soccer player Cruz. Iván insists he is straight while flirting heavily with Patrick, kicking off a chaotic triangle when Cruz also develops feelings for Patrick.

Elsewhere, Omar takes in a homeless teen named Bilal, and Benjamín forces the students to vote on expelling one of their own after they refuse drug tests. Omar votes against Samuel, getting him suspended for three days and shattering their friendship.

## A Body in the Lake and a Staged Confession

During a lakeside fundraiser, Omar dives into the water thinking Bilal is drowning, only to pull up Armando's decomposing body. Furious that Samuel, Rebe, and Guzman hid the murder from him, Omar helps Rebe and Bilal move the body to the beach before Samuel eventually calls the police.

Around the same time, Mencía discovers a hidden SIM card in her phone planted by Armando before he died, containing blackmail material linking Armando and Benjamín to powerful figures. When police raid Benjamín's office and catch Mencía trying to hide a second phone for her father, Samuel bursts in and confesses to Armando's murder himself, accepting Benjamín's secret promise to take care of his future if he takes the fall.

Samuel soon realizes Benjamín has no intention of paying his bail. Instead, Iván steps up to fund Samuel's release out of sympathy for Ari, though his gesture is overshadowed when Iván catches Patrick and Cruz kissing in a pool during a Valentine's charity auction.

## Ibiza and the Night by the Pool

Determined to clear Samuel, Rebe, Cayetana, and Mencía team up to recover the incriminating SIM card. Cayetana gets fired from Las Encinas after getting caught snooping in Benjamín's office, while Mencía ultimately turns the SIM card over to Rebe to save Samuel.

Meanwhile, Isadora invites Phillipe and Iván to a party in Ibiza. After Isadora passes out, three men lock the door and assault her while Iván and Phillipe are drugged and unconscious nearby. When one of the attackers later shows Phillipe and Iván a video of the assault, Phillipe confronts him and finally admits to Isadora that he genuinely cares about her. Around the same time, Cruz cancels his move to Qatar and encourages Iván to be with Patrick.

In the finale, Ari hands Samuel the SIM card and leaves the choice up to him. Samuel decides to turn it over to the police, rejecting Benjamín's last-minute bribe of an Oxford scholarship and monthly allowance. When Benjamín tries to grab the bag back, the two struggle by the pool. Samuel slips, cracks his head on the concrete edge, and falls bleeding into the water while Benjamín freezes.

Patrick arrives, pulls Samuel from the pool, and calls for help. Barely conscious, Samuel tells everyone that Benjamín attacked him before the police arrive and arrest Benjamín in front of his children.

## Where We Left Everyone

* **Samuel:** Suffers a severe head injury during his struggle with Benjamín and nearly drowns, surviving long enough to name Benjamín as his attacker.
* **Benjamín:** Arrested in handcuffs by the pool in front of his three children.
* **Patrick:** Pulls Samuel from the water and watches his father get taken into custody.
* **Iván:** Stays in Madrid after Cruz steps aside and encourages him to pursue Patrick.
* **Cruz:** Cancels his move to Qatar and gives Patrick and Iván his blessing.
* **Rebe & Mencía:** Mencía hands over the SIM card to save Samuel, then pulls Rebe off Benjamín after the attack.
* **Ari:** Hands Samuel the SIM card and watches her father get arrested.
* **Omar:** Reconciles with the group after finding Armando's body and walks away from the finale with Rebe.
* **Cayetana:** Fired from her janitorial job after breaking into Benjamín's office to help Samuel.
* **Isadora:** Survives the assault in Ibiza and ends the season supported by Phillipe.
