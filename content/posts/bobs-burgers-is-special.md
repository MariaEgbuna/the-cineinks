Most long running shows lose steam the longer they go. You can just feel the writers running out of gas by a certain point. Bob's Burgers is not that show, it's gotten better with time.

It's been on since January 2011, and it just wrapped up season 16 this year, which included the show's 300th episode. Fox already locked it in through season 19, so this thing is running until at least 2028. I started watching the 16th season, just one episode in, and I got hit with that same warm feeling you get revisiting an old favourite.

* * *

### Meet the Belchers

![Bob's Burgers is Special](/images/posts/bobs-burgers-is-special/image-2.jpg)

Before I get into why I love this show so much, quick rundown for anyone who hasn't seen it. Bob's Burgers follows the Belcher family: Bob and Linda, and their three kids, Tina, Gene, and Louise. They run a burger shop, it's usually broke or barely surviving, and most episodes are just them dealing with regular family stuff, customers, money problems, and each other. Sounds simple, but that's kind of the point. The show isn't leaning on some big gimmick to work. It's just a family you actually enjoy spending time with.

### Why this show feels different.

What makes this show stand out to me is the family dynamic. The Belchers really care about each other and you can feel it in almost every episode. Sure the kids do dumb stuff sometimes, but honestly that's what makes it feel real. Kids do dumb stuff in real life too. Nobody in this family is out here tearing each other down for laughs.

Compare that to Meg getting dumped on constantly in Family Guy, or Bart getting choked out by Homer in the Simpsons. Those shows get their laughs a different way, and that's fine, but Bob's Burgers proves you can be funny without the family hurting each other to get there. Keeping that up for this long is genuinely hard to do, and it's part of why the show has picked up two Emmys for Outstanding Animated Program along the way, back in 2014 and 2017.

### Bob is the anchor

![Bob's Burgers is Special](/images/posts/bobs-burgers-is-special/image-3.jpg)

Bob is the dad, and my favourite character on the show. He's a good father, a good husband, and just a good person, and the show never lets you forget it. He gets frustrated sometimes when life piles on, but it never feels like a cheap joke, it feels earned. He's involved with his kids and guides them without smothering them, which is a hard balance to write well.

What makes him hit different is you know he had a rough childhood himself, and instead of repeating that with his own kids, he's spends the whole series trying to make sure they turn out okay. He also does not play about his wife, Linda. Watching him show up for his family, consistently, season after season, is honestly kind of rare for a sitcom dad.

### Linda is the spark

![Bob's Burgers is Special](/images/posts/bobs-burgers-is-special/image-4.jpg)

Linda is the mom, and she is fantastic. Loud, unorthodox, silly, but underneath all of that she's got more heart than almost anyone else on the show. She's the type to put everyone else before herself, sometimes to a fault, and that's usually the moment Bob steps in to shut it down and remind her to take care of herself too. That back and forth between the two of them, Bob's steadiness and Linda's chaos, is basically what makes the whole show run. Neither one works as well without the other.

### The kids get to be kids

![Bob's Burgers is Special](/images/posts/bobs-burgers-is-special/image-5.jpg)

Louise, Gene, and Tina are so silly and I love every bit of it. Tina is the shy, awkward one who also happens to write incredible fan fiction on the side. Gene is a bit of everything: a singer who isn't great at it, a keyboard player who also isn't great at it, and someone who dabbles in acting too. Louise is the firecracker of the group, and she clearly gets that energy straight from Linda.

They're so chaotic, but I still love them because they're kids. They're still figuring things out, and the show lets them mess up without turning it into a big lesson every single time.

Quick shoutout too to Teddy, the landlord, Gayle, the mortician next door, and Jimmy Pesto and his kids. They add so much texture to this world without ever stealing the spotlight from the Belchers.

### My only complaint

I have to get this off my chest though. The scheduling on this show is rough. You don't get a new episode every week like a normal show. Sometimes you'll get one, then wait two weeks for the next one, and it messes with the momentum of watching. Season 16 is a good example: it started in September 2025, went on a break after the midseason finale in late December, and didn't come back until the end of April! That's a four month gap in the middle of a single season.

And it's FOX's fault! They split their Sunday animation lineup between a few shows: Bob's Burgers, the Simpsons, Family Guy, and now newer stuff like Krapopolis and Universal Basic Guys. When you're juggling that many shows in one block, someone's schedule ends up getting shuffled around to make room, and Bob's Burgers tends to be the one that takes the hit. It's not that the show is falling apart behind the scenes, it's more of a network traffic problem than a Bob's Burgers problem.

Still, it's annoying as a viewer. You can't really settle into a weekly rhythm with it the way you can with other shows, and I think that hurts how casual fans keep up with it, even if the people who stick around, like me, don't mind waiting.

* * *

At the end of the day I love this show because this family always has each other's back. Always. And sometimes that's really all I want from a TV show. If you haven't watched Bob's Burgers yet, you're seriously missing out on one of the most wholesome family shows on TV.
