**Hope** is the latest film from Na Hong-jin, the director of *The Wailing*, and it competed for the Palme d'Or at Cannes in May. It runs around 160 minutes and stars Hwang Jung-min, Zo In-sung and Hoyeon, with Michael Fassbender and Alicia Vikander in the cast too.

## The Premise

The movie is set in Hope Harbor, a remote village near the heavily fortified DMZ. Police chief Bum-seok and officer Sung-ae get called out when a mysterious creature wrecks the village, and a group of hunters, including Sung-ki, goes into the forest to track it and ends up being hunted instead.

The first hour plays like a grounded horror mystery. A creature is tearing through a small village, the hunters head into the forest, and for a while it feels like a very tense creature movie. Then, after the first hour, it goes full chaotic sci-fi, and it keeps escalating until the end.

## What Didn't Work

**The movie doesn't explain much**: Most of what I understand about this movie, I picked up online. Na has said the original script was too long and too dense for a single movie, so he cut it down. That's why he ended up explaining so much himself in interviews.

**It's a lot**: About an hour in, this movie goes completely off the rails. It's a lot. I know it divided people at Cannes, but I never minded.

## What Did

Everything else.

## Should you watch it?

If you like your movies big and a little unhinged, watch this one. Bring someone to react with, because you'll want to talk about it right after. I watched this movie twice, and I'd do it again.

Na actually said a sequel script is already written, but nothing's confirmed, so we'll see.
