These are seven K-dramas I started and never finished, and each one lost me for a different reason.

Some had stories I couldn't stand. Some weren't what I expected. A couple are big hits that I couldn't get into. And one is still on my watchlist.

## When the story stopped working

![I Keep Dropping Kdramas Halfway Through](/images/posts/unfinished-k-dramas/not-interesting.jpg)

**Marry My Husband** (2024) is a revenge fantasy based on a web novel, with Park Min-young and Na In-woo in the lead roles. Kang Ji-won wakes up ten years in the past after her husband and best friend betray her, and she sets out to change her fate. That is a strong setup on paper, but the story felt so messy and predictable that I couldn't stand it any longer. I quit around episode 8 and never looked back.

**If Wishes Could Kill** (2026) is Netflix's first Korean young adult horror series, directed by Park Yoon-seo, who co-directed *Moving*. Five friends at Seorin High get caught in a curse that spreads through a wish-granting app called Girigo, which is a modern take on the monkey's paw idea. To me it tries so hard to be edgy, the characters weren't interesting enough, and the story was just all over the place. I gave it 6 of its 8 episodes before I stopped.

## Not what I signed up for

![I Keep Dropping Kdramas Halfway Through](/images/posts/unfinished-k-dramas/dropped-shows.jpg)

**Study Group** (2025) is a series based on a webtoon, with Hwang Min-hyun as Yoon Ga-min, a student with a talent for fighting who starts a study group at a school known as the worst around. It was a hit on Tving and made Viki's top five in 147 countries. I never knew the show would so... goofy and over the top. These students could punch through walls, someone else jumped off the third floor and landing on their feet. It's billed as an action comedy, so that part is by design, but it wasn't the show I went in expecting, so I never bothered to finish it.

**Psychopath Diary** (2019). A dark comedy thriller starring Yoon Shi-yoon as Yook Dong-sik, a timid office worker who had a small amnesia. He finds a serial killer's diary and starts believing he's the killer. The silliness is part of the premise, but it got to be too much for me. I was tricked by TikTok edits. I thought it was a BL. I stayed for a few episodes and stopped around episode 7.

## Big hits that couldn't keep me

![I Keep Dropping Kdramas Halfway Through](/images/posts/unfinished-k-dramas/image-5.jpg)

**Reply 1988** (2015) is a show about five families and their teenage kids in a Seoul neighborhood in the late 1980s. I only watched two episodes. A shame, because this show is very popular and I really tried to like it. But, the goat sounds? No. Just no. Also, I could already tell there would probably be a love triangle, and I hate those, so I dropped it. 

**The Fiery Priest** (2019). It's an crime comedy where Kim Nam-gil plays Kim Hae-il, a priest with anger issues who teams up with a cowardly detective to solve a murder. I have no reason for dropping this show. I just stopped watching one day, and never went back.

## The one I still owe a finish

![I Keep Dropping Kdramas Halfway Through](/images/posts/unfinished-k-dramas/navillera.jpg)

**Navillera** (2021) is a 12-episode tvN drama based on a webtoon. Park In-hwan plays Shim Deok-chul, a 70-year-old retired mailman chasing his lifelong dream of learning ballet, and Song Kang plays Lee Chae-rok, the 23-year-old dancer who ends up teaching him. It's different from the rest of this list because it's still on my watchlist. I must finish it. I just don't know why I keep falling asleep.

---

Navillera is the only one on this list I plan to go back to. It's 12 episodes, so not a big ask. This time, I'm finishing it.
