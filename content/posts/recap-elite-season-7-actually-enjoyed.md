Dropping the format of flash-forward interrogations, Elite Season 7 shifts toward linear storytelling while tackling heavy arcs involving domestic abuse, undercover policing, and mental health. The season centers on shifting power dynamics at Las Encinas before culminating in Raúl's death and a dangerous cover-up.

_This post contains spoilers for Season 7 of Netflix's Elite._

_Content note: this recap mentions sexual assault, domestic abuse, suicide ideation, and murder._

---

### The new director's secret

![Season 7 Recap](/images/posts/recap-elite-season-7/image-1.jpg)

The season opens with Omar in therapy. His counselor, Mateo, pushes him to process his grief over Samuel, but the nightmares haven't stopped.

At Las Encinas, panic's spreading over Didac. His family's mafia ties put him at the center of suspicion for last season's shooting, and worried parents push for a vote on whether he can stay. The new director, Luis, tries to smooth things over, but Didac's suspended while the vote plays out. His brother, Pau, tells him to stay out of it and promises he isn't going anywhere. Nico wants to help him, though Rocío isn't sure it's worth the trouble.

A new student, Chloe, becomes the school's latest gossip target when her sex tape leaks. She brushes off Sonia's concern, and it comes out that she leaked it herself. She tells her mother, Carmen, that everyone's going to use people like them, so she might as well control the game. Carmen has her own history. Her contact Jose Luis turns out to be Cruz's lawyer, and she once signed away access to her son in exchange for a payout from Cruz. Now she's trying to reopen that deal.

Ivan's discharged from the hospital, and the group throws him a surprise party. Sara's eaten up with guilt, and Raúl quietly plants the idea that Patrick might've been the one who hit Ivan. Ivan doesn't buy it. Just as Sara's about to confess, Raúl stops her by hurting her hand. Once they're alone, he offers to take the blame for the accident himself, then comforts her over the "sacrifice" he's making for her.

Luis guilts Nico's parents, María and Alfonso, into letting Didac stay. In the middle of that conversation, they learn María's nephew, Eric, has been arrested at a protest that turned into a riot. They bail him out and invite him to stay with them. Alfonso worries Eric's activism will be a bad influence and points to Didac as the kind of friend Nico needs instead.

At Ivan's, the group tries to lighten the mood. Ivan tells Isadora, who's avoiding Didac, not to mourn what's lost but to make the most of the time she has with people. Patrick leaves Ivan a voicemail before officially leaving, promising it isn't goodbye and that he still loves him. Once everyone's gone, Ivan breaks down crying until a pizza delivery guy, Joel, arrives. Ivan trips on his way to the door, Joel catches him, and he stays for dinner.

Sara tries to go see Isadora, but Raúl locks the doors and blames her for provoking the earlier fight that left her bruised. They end up watching a movie, Sara miserable while Raúl dozes off beside her.

At the club, Nico apologizes to Sonia over how things went with Ari, and she tells him she accepts him as he is. Chloe lets Eric comfort her about the leaked tape and even shows it to him. She kisses him, he says he'd rather get to know her first, and she talks him into sleeping with her anyway, secretly recording it.

Back at Ivan's, Joel comforts him through the loneliness and they kiss, but Joel pulls back, unsure it's the right move. Isadora invites Didac up to her suite, which catches her mother Roberta's attention, while Pau pulls Isadora aside separately to talk. Isadora and Didac share an honest moment over the uncertainty hanging over him, and it turns physical until Isadora dissociates and asks him to stop. He stays with her, worried. Elsewhere, Nico tries to kiss Sonia, but she deflects and asks to just dance.

Ivan apologizes to Joel, and they agree to be friends. Ivan even offers to use part of his inheritance to help Joel's future. At the club, Eric tries to get Chloe to have a real conversation and she walks off. She later watches the recording with him, and when Sonia warns her to be careful with it, Chloe admits she's the one who leaked her own tape. At home, Carmen tells her to have more self-respect, and Chloe pushes back, saying she's playing the same game her mother taught her.

Jose Luis and Carmen meet again. She's demanding access to Cruz's pension, tied to the contract she signed agreeing to stay away from her son. If he won't pay, she'll break that agreement and reach out to Ivan directly. The scene cuts to Ivan looking at an old photo of his father.

At home, Joel gets a package from Ivan: a full scholarship offer to Las Encinas. Omar wakes up beside him and is stunned to see it. He'd told Joel he studied in the US rather than admit the truth, and now he finally comes clean about what the school did to him and the people he loves. Joel argues it isn't fair to deny him the chance, and Omar, scared but supportive, agrees to back him.

When Joel shows up to register, Ivan's thrilled to show him around. Omar turns up too, finally accepting the internship offer to help run the school's app. María and Roberta agree to let Didac stay, which Luis calls the right call. The episode ends with Luis, revealed to be an undercover cop, pinning Didac's photo up on an investigation board.

### Sara plays the long game

![Season 7 Recap](/images/posts/recap-elite-season-7/image-2.jpg)

The school's anonymous app becomes a weapon almost immediately, with students piling onto Didac. Omar quietly spirals, unsettled by how close Ivan and Joel are getting and increasingly sure therapy isn't helping. He has a panic attack reliving the night Samuel died. Ivan buys Joel a suit for an upcoming race, and when Omar finally meets Joel in person, Ivan introduces him as his boyfriend.

Roberta turns out to be behind the shooting that closed out last season, a secret Pau lets slip in a flashback. She makes her disapproval of Isadora and Didac's relationship clear from the start. When Didac comforts Isadora through her struggles with intimacy, Roberta interrupts to pressure Isadora into entering the family's horse in an upcoming race, over her objections. The horse is poisoned not long after, and Roberta immediately blames Pau.

Sara's unraveling under Raúl's control becomes impossible to hide. She breaks out in stress rashes she scratches raw and claims are allergies. Her PE teacher, Jessica, notices and starts pushing her toward a self-defense class. Meanwhile Chloe, unaware Ivan's her half brother, starts eyeing him as her next conquest until Carmen steps in and reveals the truth.

Sara is playing a longer game than anyone realizes. She convinces Raúl that getting Ivan to move in with them would help control the narrative around the hit and run and bring in rent money. Around the same time, anonymous cries for help start appearing on the school app, and it's revealed Omar has his own history with suicidal ideation, a moment in his past when Joel talked him back from a ledge.

Sara's birthday party becomes the backdrop for Ivan moving in. Luis secretly hacks into Didac's phone under the guise of a hug. Rocío, Nico, and Eric join a protest at a homeless shelter that turns violent, ending in a gunshot and Rocío's arrest while Eric panics and runs. Sonia gives Nico another chance after a mixtape wins her over, but Chloe, chasing Sonia's attention out of spite, sleeps with Nico and gets caught in the act. Sonia calls her out for using people as pawns. Raúl proposes to Sara live on stream. She doesn't answer, and shows up to Jessica's self-defense class instead.

Eric's mental health spirals in the episodes that follow. He self-harms and swings between warmth and shutdown, and a semicolon tattoo hints at a history nobody's addressed. At a school exchange event, Ivan, still grieving his father, decides to apply for the program just to get away. Chloe and Eric's connection keeps breaking down whenever either gets too close to being honest. Martin, Isadora's father, bribes Judge Duran over an eviction while Luis listens in. Drug dealers are raided at the club not long after, and it comes out that Pau orchestrated it.

Omar tells Joel to stop holding back and go be with Ivan, insisting he'll be fine alone. He isn't. Dalmar finds him passed out surrounded by pills and empty bottles, and Joel moves in to keep watch over him.

As Omar recovers, Sara pulls Chloe into her scheme, using her to stage a threesome with Raúl purely to catch him on camera and get real leverage. In the middle of that, Ivan and Joel are caught sleeping together in Omar's own bed. Omar's gutted, and Ivan, guilt-ridden, offers to end things if that's what Omar needs. Meanwhile, high on cocaine with Carmen while sorting through Cruz's belongings, Ivan watches her hallucinate Cruz mid-conversation. She kisses him, realizes what she's done, and flees in horror, a moment made worse by a truth Ivan doesn't know yet. Isadora, pulled deeper into her father's orbit, is offered ownership of the family club, and Luis moves her from a background figure to a central name on his board.

Sara turns on Chloe publicly to protect her own image, and whatever trust they'd built fractures. Isadora, manipulated by her father into signing off on an eviction that targets her own friends' building, is talked out of it by Didac before real damage is done. Cornered by Raúl, Sara finally turns the tables and threatens mutual destruction if he goes to the police, leaving an uneasy standoff. Eric's spiral bottoms out in an incident that ends with an ultimatum from Nico's family: therapy or nothing. Carmen begs Ivan to keep what happened between them secret, unaware Chloe's already overheard.

Omar chooses himself, moving out and back in with his parents and leaving Joel in tears over an empty room. Ivan and Joel commit to each other. Isadora, growing suspicious of Didac, secretly installs cameras and catches him photographing documents meant to expose her family, the betrayal Luis had been engineering all along. Ivan, done being used, confronts Carmen and coldly negotiates a two million euro payout in exchange for cutting all ties.

### A fall from the roof

![Season 7 Recap](/images/posts/recap-elite-season-7/image-3.jpg)

The finale opens with police finding a body and ruling it a suicide before the show says whose. Elsewhere, Nico's family panics when Eric goes missing. When he comes home high, a frustrated Nico snaps and throws him out.

Isadora confronts Didac over Luis, and the two end up at the police station, where Luis admits he went undercover after last season's shooting. Isadora tells him she won't betray the people she loves for his investigation.

Sara watches Raúl and Chloe together in the halls, and Jessica tells her what's happening: Raúl knows Sara's still hooked on him and will keep dangling that control for as long as she lets him. The only real way out is to leave the school. Instead, Sara lures Raúl into sleeping with her one more time and secretly records it. Carmen, with Ivan's payout in hand, tries to reach Chloe for a fresh start, only to find Raúl's blocked her number "for Chloe's own good." Sara catches Carmen in the hallway and warns her about Raúl's history, how he isolates and invalidates before the abuse even starts. Then she sends Chloe the tape.

Eric, convinced everyone's given up on him after Nico's outburst, ends up alone at the squat house. He climbs to the roof and posts on the self-help app that he wants to talk before he ends his life. Sonia's the one who answers.

Isadora, still wrestling with what to do about her family, is pushed by Rocío's blunt insistence that people who do wrong should face consequences. She confronts her parents, only to learn they already know about Luis's investigation and aren't worried, since the police don't have real evidence yet. She realizes the shooting and her horse's death were both her own family's doing, part of a power play against Didac's. She hands the trafficking documents to Judge Duran, enraging her father, while Roberta, oddly sympathetic, tells her this is why she wanted her daughter kept out of the family business.

Ivan asks Joel to come with him to South Africa. Joel turns to Omar and Dalmar for advice. Omar tells him to go if he loves Ivan. Dalmar tells him to stop letting men run his life and live for himself.

Chloe notices Ivan's bags packed and gets pulled away by Raúl before she can process it, right as Sara's tape arrives in her inbox. Furious, she slaps him and tries to leave. He throws her phone and hits her. Carmen shows up outside, refusing to leave until the door opens, and Raúl warns Chloe to keep it together or he'll lose control. She fakes calm, but Carmen sees the fear on her face and the broken phone on the floor. As Ivan arrives to grab his things before the airport, Carmen quietly tells Chloe to leave with him, then invites Raúl up to the rooftop for a drink.

Isadora sets her own trap. She tells Roberta not to come to the meeting with Martin and lies to her father that her mother's lost trust in her. Martin, thinking his plan's working, tells Isadora she'll never be cut out for the family business, since his real business with Duran is finished and she's no longer useful. As Duran texts that she's arrived at the meeting location, Isadora forwards her location to the police, agrees with her father that she should put the business first, then reveals she's been wearing a wire the whole time. The police, having heard everything, arrest Martin and race toward Duran.

Sonia realizes it's Eric on the other end of the app conversation and tells Nico, who runs to the squat house roof and reaches him. Nico admits his earlier anger was really at himself for not knowing how to help, tells Eric how much he's changed his life, and the two embrace as Eric agrees to come home.

At the airport, Ivan waits for Joel, who shows up bag in hand, uncertain until the moment arrives. Back home, Dalmar looks visibly anxious while Omar wonders what's wrong. In the end, Joel doesn't get on the plane. He returns home heartbroken, and Omar gently tells him staying apart for now is for the best, then goes back to therapy himself.

On the rooftop, Duran waits alone until Rocío joins her. Martin's men arrive with guns and drag both women toward the edge. Police storm in just in time, arresting the men and taking Duran into custody for her own corruption, leaving Rocío devastated.

On the other rooftop, Carmen confronts Raúl over hitting Chloe, needling him until he snaps and chokes her. She asks, almost calmly, for ice for her whiskey. Confused enough to loosen his grip, he lets her go and scoffs at how unhinged she seems. She asks again what a man's supposed to do when a woman wants ice in her drink. While he's still processing the question, she shoves him off the roof, telling him to go get it himself. She looks down and finds him dead below.

The season closes on the fallout. Nico brings Eric home to his parents' relief. Roberta is stunned by everything that's come out, and Isadora pretends total ignorance about her father's arrest, telling Didac to stay away from her instead of explaining. Chloe and Carmen are both questioned once Raúl's body is found, with Carmen telling officers he was a troubled abuser and that Chloe was with her the whole time. In the final scene, a flashback reveals Dalmar was standing near the building when Raúl fell, and the footage on his phone shows Carmen watching from the rooftop right after. He stares at the video, terrified.

### Where we left everyone

* **Raúl:** Dead. Carmen pushed him off a rooftop. The police are calling it suicide for now.
* **Carmen:** Covered by Chloe's alibi. She has Ivan's two million, and Dalmar's video is out there.
* **Sara:** Free of Raúl. She recorded him, baited him, and he died before he could retaliate.
* **Chloe:** Lied to the police for her mother. Her relationship with Carmen is unresolved.
* **Ivan:** On a plane to South Africa. His story with Joel is open-ended.
* **Joel:** Stayed behind. He went home to Omar and Dalmar.
* **Omar:** Back in therapy and out of Joel's space.
* **Eric:** Home and safe, thanks to Nico.
* **Nico:** Threw Eric out, then reached him on the roof in time.
* **Isadora:** Her father's under arrest and the corruption case is closed. She and Didac part ways.
* **Didac:** Still in love with Isadora. His cooperation with Luis paid off but cost him everything else.
* **Rocío:** Her mother was arrested for corruption.
* **Luis:** Case closed. Martin and Duran are both in custody.
* **Dalmar:** Holding video evidence of the night Raúl died. He hasn't decided what to do with it.
