Dropping the format of flash-forward interrogations, *Elite* Season 7 shifts toward linear storytelling while tackling heavy arcs involving domestic abuse, undercover policing, and mental health. The season centers on shifting power dynamics at Las Encinas before culminating in Raúl's death and a dangerous cover-up.

*This post contains spoilers for Season 7 of Netflix's Elite.*

*Content note: this recap mentions sexual assault, domestic abuse, suicide ideation, and murder.*

### The Quick Version (TL;DR)
* **The Return & New Faces:** Omar returns to Las Encinas in therapy over Samuel's death while his boyfriend, Joel, catches Iván's eye, and manipulative newcomers Chloe and her mother, Carmen, shake up the hallways.
* **The Undercover Sting:** New school director Luis is secretly an undercover cop investigating the mafia ties behind last season's drive-by shooting, pressuring Didac to spy on Isadora's family.
* **The Finale:** Isadora wears a wire to expose her father's corruption, Sara leaks a video that turns Raúl violent against Chloe, and Carmen pushes Raúl off a rooftop while Dalmar accidentally films the aftermath.

---

## A Familiar Face

![Season 7 Recap](/images/posts/recap-elite-season-7/image-1.jpg)

The season opens with Omar in therapy, haunted by nightmares over Samuel's death, while his boyfriend, Joel, takes a pizza delivery job that leads him straight to a grieving Iván. Touched by Joel's kindness, Iván funds a full scholarship for Joel to attend Las Encinas, prompting Omar to accept an internship running the school's anonymous app so he can stay close.

Meanwhile, new director Luis is secretly an undercover police officer investigating last season's drive-by shooting, which was orchestrated by Isadora's mother, Roberta, in a mafia turf war against Didac's family. Several new dynamics quickly disrupt the school:
* **Chloe & Carmen:** Chloe leaks her own sex tape to control her reputation, unaware that Iván is her biological half-brother until her mother, Carmen, reveals she once signed away parental rights to Iván for a payout from Cruz.
* **Eric:** Nico's activist cousin moves in with his family after getting arrested at a riot, masking severe depression and self-harm behind reckless behavior.
* **Sara & Raúl:** Raúl continues to isolate and abuse Sara while holding last season's hit-and-run over her head.

## Shifting Loyalties and Breaking Points

![Season 7 Recap](/images/posts/recap-elite-season-7/image-2.jpg)

As the semester unfolds, every relationship fractures under pressure. Joel and Iván fall for each other, devastating Omar when he catches them together in his own bed. After surviving an overdose scare, Omar finally chooses himself, moving back in with his parents so he can focus on healing.

Elsewhere, Carmen spirals on drugs while sorting through Cruz's belongings and accidentally kisses Iván, prompting Iván to demand a two-million-euro payout from her to cut ties permanently. Luis manipulates Didac into photographing incriminating documents at Isadora's home, though Isadora catches him on security cameras and ends their relationship.

Sara finally stops playing victim and goes on the offensive. After taking self-defense classes, she recruits Chloe into a staged threesome with Raúl to record his behavior, then flips the script when Raúl starts pursuing Chloe instead.

## A Fall from the Roof

![Season 7 Recap](/images/posts/recap-elite-season-7/image-3.jpg)

Everything collides in the finale. After Nico kicks Eric out following a drug relapse, Eric climbs to the roof of a squat house and posts a cry for help on the school app. Sonia recognizes him on the chat and alerts Nico, who races to the roof and talks Eric down just in time.

Meanwhile, Isadora discovers her father, Martín, is bribing Judge Durán (Rocío's mother) over a corrupt eviction deal. Wearing a police wire to a meeting with Martín, Isadora gets his confession on tape, leading to the arrests of both Martín and Judge Durán.

At the same time, Sara lures Raúl into sleeping with her one more time, records it, and sends the footage to Chloe. When Chloe confronts Raúl and tries to leave, he turns violent. Carmen arrives, sees the bruises on her daughter's face, and calmly invites Raúl up to the rooftop for a drink. When Raúl snaps and chokes her, Carmen tricks him into letting go and shoves him off the roof to his death.

Police rule Raúl's death a suicide after Chloe gives her mother an alibi. However, a final flashback reveals that Dalmar was standing on the street below when Raúl fell, capturing video on his phone of Carmen watching from the rooftop edge.

## Where We Left Everyone

* **Raúl:** Dead after Carmen pushes him off a rooftop, with police initially ruling it a suicide.
* **Carmen:** Protected by Chloe's alibi and holding Iván's two million euros, unaware Dalmar has video of the murder.
* **Sara:** Finally free of Raúl after exposing him to Chloe.
* **Chloe:** Lied to the police to protect her mother, leaving their relationship strained.
* **Iván:** Boards a flight to South Africa for an exchange program, leaving his future with Joel uncertain.
* **Joel:** Turns down Iván's offer to leave Spain and returns home to Omar and Dalmar.
* **Omar:** Returns to therapy and steps back from Joel to focus on his own recovery.
* **Eric & Nico:** Reconciled and safe after Nico saves Eric on the roof.
* **Isadora & Didac:** Isadora puts her corrupt father behind bars, though she cuts Didac off over his undercover betrayal.
* **Rocío:** Devastated after her mother, Judge Durán, is arrested for corruption.
* **Luis:** Closes his undercover investigation with Martín and Durán in custody.
* **Dalmar:** Holds incriminating video evidence from the night Raúl died.
