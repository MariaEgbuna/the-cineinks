*One Piece* has been running since 1999, which is bonkers. I have never seen it, and I don't plan to. Ever.

It has over 1,000 episodes, and it *still* has fans. I know the premise, or at least I think I do. What I didn't know was how goofy the show is. I watched a clip on TikTok and, my word! How and why are people still watching this? The animation is so silly, they take forever to do one basic task, and yet the fans eat it up every time.

Then there's how the female characters are drawn, which is just egregious. The jigglewatts on their boobs are terrible. She breathes, it shakes. She laughs, it shakes. She moves her arm, it shakes. The blonde guy is kinda weird too, and the art style is ugly. 

To be fair (barely), all of this comes from clips I've seen on social media.

A friend told me to watch the first few *hundred* episodes and I'll get it. No. No, I will not. The only long series I will watch is *Gintama*, and I still haven't finished it.

I've also heard there's a trimmed version called *One Pace*, a fan edit for people who want to skip the filler and the dragging. I don't care.

Maybe I'm wrong (I am not), but at what point do the fans admit the show is weird?

This has unfortunately turned into a hate post. How can I hate something I've never watched? Apparently, like this.
