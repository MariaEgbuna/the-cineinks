**The Affair Was Just the Beginning** is an eight-episode black comedy thriller that premiered on Coupang Play in July 2026. Kim Hye-soo plays Kyung-hee, an interior design CEO and social media influencer, and Kim Ji-hoon plays her husband, Jae-hong, with Cho Yeo-jeong and Kim Jae-chul in the main cast too. The setup is simple: Kyung-hee suspects Jae-hong is cheating, and the show wants you to think that's the whole story.

![The cast of The Affair Was Just the Beginning (2026)](/images/posts/review-the-affair-was-just-the-beginning/cast.jpg)

*A quick heads up: this review has minor spoilers for the direction the plot takes, but nothing about how it ends.*

## The Highlights

* **Seeing Kim Ji-hoon outside the villain box.** It's rare to see him in anything other than a villain role. The man drove me crazy as Baek Hee-sung in *Flower of Evil* (2020) and as Choi Pro in *Ballerina* (2023), so it took me a while to get used to him here. (*Flower of Evil* is on my [K-drama recommendations](/posts/kdrama-recommendations-may-27-2026).)
* **Chaos that keeps bubbling until it explodes.** I'm a fan of chaotic comedy, the kind where everything keeps piling up until the whole thing blows. This show does that well. The suspicion of an affair quickly turns into a hit-and-run, a hidden body, a family fighting to survive, and an anonymous blackmailer. The story is told from multiple viewpoints, and every new perspective reframes what you thought you knew and peels back another layer of absurdity. It's nothing like the usual formulaic K-dramas.

## Fair Warning

* **The ending feels a little predictable.** The final episodes also move heavily away from the original setup into frantic chaos, and I missed the tension the early episodes had.
* **The set looks cheap.** Some scenes look like they were filmed in one room, and the green screen background was so obvious in a few moments.

---

It's eight episodes, so start it without reading too much about it and let it get out of hand.
