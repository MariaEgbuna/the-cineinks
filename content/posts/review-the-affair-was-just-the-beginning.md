The Affair Was Just The Beginning is one of the best written Korean shows of 2026, and I loved every unhinged minute of it.

It's an eight-episode black comedy crime thriller that premiered on Coupang Play on July 31, 2026. Lee Chang-hee directed it, and Jung Eun-kyung and Park Soo-rin wrote it. Kim Hye-soo and Kim Ji-hoon lead, with Cho Yeo-jeong and Kim Jae-chul in the cast. I watched it for Kim Ji-hoon.

![REVIEW: The Affair Was Just The Beginning (2026)](/images/posts/review-the-affair-was-just-the-beginning/image-1.jpg)

Kyung-hee (Kim Hye-soo) is a successful interior design CEO and social media influencer. Her husband Jae-hong (Kim Ji-hoon) is an actor living in her shadow. It opens like a standard cheating spouse drama, with Kyung-hee suspecting her husband, and then it spirals into a hit-and-run accident, a hidden body, and an anonymous blackmailer.

**This review has minor spoilers.** I stick to the setup and the general direction of the story, and I don't give away how it ends.

## What I loved about it

* **Kim Ji-hoon, without the villain face:** It's rare to see Kim Ji-hoon outside his usual villain roles, and it took me a while to get used to it. The man drove me crazy as Baek Hee-sung in *Flower of Evil* (2020) and as Choi Pro in *Ballerina* (2023), so watching him as Jae-hong took some adjusting, but I loved it. He was charming, funny, and a little pathetic, and he made me root for him even when he was doing the wrong thing.

* **Chaos that keeps bubbling:** I'm a fan of chaotic comedy, where everything keeps bubbling until it explodes, and this show does it well. The story is told from multiple viewpoints, and every new perspective reframes what you just saw and peels back another layer of absurdity. It's nothing like the usual formulaic K-dramas.

## My only complaints

* The ending isn't perfect. It just felt a little predictable, and the final episodes move heavily away from the initial setup into frantic chaos.
* I also didn't like "the set." It looks like they stayed in one room to film some scenes, and the green screen background was so obvious in a few moments.

## Final Verdict

**My Rating: 8.4**

It's eight episodes, so start it without reading too much about it and let it get out of hand. The affair *really* was just the beginning.
